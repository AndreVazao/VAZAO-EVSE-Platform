# M2.9-V — IEC 61010-2-030 edition and measuring-circuit scope

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** APPLICABILITY CANDIDATE — PRODUCT CLASSIFICATION STILL OPEN  
**Parent:** M2.9-T standards applicability; M2.9-U MEE1 exclusion

## 1. Verified IEC publication

The IEC Webstore lists **IEC 61010-2-030:2023**, titled “Safety requirements for electrical equipment for measurement, control, and laboratory use — Part 2-030: Particular requirements for equipment having testing or measuring circuits”.

Official IEC catalogue page: https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/75915

Its published scope is equipment with testing or measuring circuits connected, for test or measurement, to devices or circuits outside the measurement equipment itself. It explicitly includes measurement circuits that form part of electrical test/measurement equipment, laboratory equipment or process-control equipment, with additional protective means between the circuit and operator.

This description is directly relevant to the TestStation architecture if its voltage/current sensing inputs connect to external EVSE or mains-related circuits. It is therefore a strong candidate to assess together with IEC 61010-1, not a final declaration of applicability.

## 2. Edition register correction

M2.9-T identified IEC 61010-2-030 as a candidate but did not freeze an edition. The current IEC catalogue result verifies the 2023 edition. The standards register should therefore use IEC 61010-2-030:2023 as the edition to assess, subject to confirmation of applicable European/national adoption and any current amendments/corrigenda.

For the general requirements, the IEC catalogue currently exposes IEC 61010-1:2010 with AMD1:2016 consolidated text (official catalogue: https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/59769). This is the candidate general standard edition to assess; verify applicable EN adoption and corrigenda before compliance planning.

For insulation coordination, IEC 60664-1:2020+AMD1:2025 consolidated edition is listed by IEC (https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/107319). This is a candidate technical basis, not an independent substitute for the applicable product standard.

## 3. TestStation relevance and boundary

The TestStation includes embedded current and voltage sensing channels intended to observe external electrical circuits. If the instrument is a standalone test/measurement product, IEC 61010-1 together with IEC 61010-2-030 is a stronger standards route than treating IEC 61851-1 (EVSE product requirements) as the instrument's sole safety standard.

If the TestStation is instead integrated into an EVSE product or a production machine/test fixture, the conformity route may differ and must be determined from the product boundary, intended use, operator access, installation and market.

## 4. Inputs still required

The 2023 edition does not itself determine the TestStation's CAT assignment without knowing intended connection points and use. The following remain mandatory inputs:
- exact circuits and terminals connected to the instrument;
- fixed installation, distribution panel, EVSE output, vehicle-side circuit, laboratory source or other connection point;
- measurement category and rated voltage assigned to every input;
- foreseeable transient/impulse environment and source impedance;
- operator access, enclosure, probes/leads/connectors and protective measures;
- pollution degree, altitude and insulation objective;
- applicable European harmonised/adopted edition and conformity route.

Do not assign CAT II/III/IV, basic/reinforced insulation or a clearance value by assumption.

## 5. Consequence for the power architecture

M2.9-U has excluded MEE1S2405SC from safety-isolation barriers based on Murata's explicit functional-insulation statement. Replacement converter selection remains blocked until the above product and measurement inputs are fixed and insulation-coordination requirements are derived.
