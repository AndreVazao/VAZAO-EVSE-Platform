**Date:** 2026-11-22  
**Status:** ENGINEERING WORKSHEET — OPEN REQUIREMENTS; NO COMPONENT OR TOPOLOGY APPROVED  
**Scope:** Six intended AMC1311B high-side domains U3–U8  
**Inputs:** M2.9-AA requirements gate and M2.9-AB manufacturer candidate screening  
**Change class:** Documentation only

## 1. Purpose and decision boundary

This matrix turns the six-channel isolated-power requirements into a traceable closure worksheet. It separates system requirements that must be derived from the product boundary from converter evidence that can only be checked after those requirements are known.

The current native MASTER/netlist evidence recorded in M2.9-AA showed U3–U8 and their associated power connections unimplemented/disconnected. Therefore no complete per-channel load, converter input envelope, or final rail voltage can be asserted here.

**No converter, output voltage, power rating, insulation class, channel grouping or architecture is selected.** Six independent floating domains remain the conservative evaluation baseline only. This worksheet does not authorize schematic/BOM/PCB changes, fabrication, energization or a safety claim.

## 2. Status vocabulary

- **OPEN — SYSTEM:** value must be derived from product boundary, hazard analysis, applicable standards and intended use.
- **OPEN — CIRCUIT:** value requires the native circuit and exact component/interface definition.
- **OPEN — SUPPLIER:** exact manufacturer datasheet, certificate/CB report or written clarification is missing.
- **VERIFY:** evidence exists but exact conditions/scope must be checked.
- **PASS:** may be assigned only with a cited controlled source and documented engineering review.
- **FAIL:** mandatory criterion demonstrably not met; candidate rejected.

No item is PASS merely because a family page, search snippet, isolation test voltage or nominal value exists.

### 5.1 Mandatory per-channel deliverables

1. Native KiCad schematic references and connected netlist proving the intended rail and return connections.
2. Exact AMC1311B order code and controlled datasheet revision; verify VDD1 operating range/current under relevant conditions.
3. Component-by-component high-side load table with reference designators, MPNs, tolerances and min/typ/max current.
4. Rail tolerance budget covering converter line/load regulation, temperature, ripple, startup, transient and ageing where applicable.
5. Worst-case power and input-current calculation, including converter efficiency and six-channel aggregation.
6. Startup, brownout, shutdown, short-circuit and recovery behaviour; define ADC/firmware detection and safe-state response.
7. Per-channel thermal estimate and six-channel aggregate enclosure/PCB assessment.
8. Isolation crossing map proving no unintended secondary path through signals, shields, connectors, test points or shared returns.

## 7. Isolation-crossing review

The AMC1311B signal barrier and its isolated power converter are parallel galvanic crossings. The assessment must include both, plus all other connections between domains.

| Crossing / path | Required check | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Low-side source to each converter input | Protection, fault energy, routing and separation | OPEN |
| Converter primary-to-secondary | Exact working/impulse rating, insulation grade and certificate | OPEN |
| AMC1311B input-to-output barrier | Exact device barrier rating and conditions | OPEN |
| Secondary-to-secondary between channels | PCB creepage/clearance, contamination and fault coupling | OPEN |
| Channel signals, shields and connectors | Identify any unintended common reference or bypass | OPEN |
| USB/Ethernet/service/debug/programming | Verify isolation or disconnection strategy in every operating mode | OPEN |
| Chassis/PE and cable screens | Define intentional bonds and fault-current paths | OPEN |

Six converter modules do not automatically create six independent safety domains if external wiring, shields, test equipment, shared returns or PCB contamination reconnects them.

## 9. Candidate-screening findings carried forward

- RECOM RHV2-2405S/R20 remains the strongest documentary candidate recorded in M2.9-AB, not a selected component. Its reinforced insulation and 1400 Vrms rated working voltage are component-level evidence only; its unregulated output and below-10% load specification caveat remain relevant.
- AMC1311B IC-only current previously calculated from TI Rev C is approximately 7.1 mA typical / 9.7 mA maximum at 4.5–5.5 V. This is not the complete channel load and is below the RHV2 40 mA 10%-load threshold.
- A post-regulator does not remove the converter minimum-load condition. A preload adds permanent dissipation and requires a full worst-case thermal and rail analysis; no preload value is selected.
- Regulated/no-minimum-load comparators screened so far did not provide adequate documented reinforced/working-voltage evidence for this intended barrier.
- Manufacturer restrictions on safety-critical use must be resolved in writing for the exact intended application where stated.

These findings are screening evidence only; re-check the current controlled manufacturer documents before relying on any value.

## 10. Closure sequence and review gates

1. Recover and audit the native KiCad MASTER, symbols, footprints, netlist and project files; do not fabricate schematic text.
2. Resolve SYS-01 through SYS-10 with the applicable engineering/safety review.
3. Trace and document the actual auxiliary source and protection envelope.
4. Reconnect/define the real U3–U8 circuits through the normal KiCad workflow, then generate and review the native netlist.
5. Complete six separate high-side load budgets and the common rail requirements.
6. Screen exact converter MPNs against every MPN gate; attach manufacturer datasheets and certificates/CB reports.
7. Review all isolation crossings, PCB spacing, thermal, EMC and fault behaviour.
8. Only after documented approval, open a separate implementation change for schematic/BOM work; run KiCad ERC and netlist/connectivity checks.
9. No fabrication or energization until the relevant design, safety and test approvals are independently complete.
