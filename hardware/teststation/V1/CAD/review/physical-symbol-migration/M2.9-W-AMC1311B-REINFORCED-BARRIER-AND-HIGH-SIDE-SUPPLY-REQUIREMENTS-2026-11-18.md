The datasheet lists safety-related certification under DIN EN IEC 60747-17 (VDE 0884-17), reinforced insulation, certificate 40040142, and UL1577 component recognition, file E181974. TI states that the device is suitable for safe electrical insulation only within its safety ratings and that compliance requires suitable protective circuits.

These component ratings are not the TestStation's system ratings. PCB layout, terminal construction, creepage/clearance, protection, enclosure and application conditions must still meet the selected equipment standard.

## 2. Critical consequence: the isolated supply is a second crossing of the barrier

The AMC1311B signal path crosses an internal reinforced capacitive isolation barrier. Its high-side supply pins VDD1/GND1 require a local floating supply, while VDD2/GND2 belong to the low-side domain.

Any isolated DC/DC converter that transfers energy from the low-side domain to VDD1/GND1 creates a second galvanic path across the same hazardous-to-safe domain boundary. This path is electrically in parallel with the amplifier's signal isolation barrier from the system safety perspective.

Therefore:
- The AMC1311B's reinforced isolation rating does not automatically confer reinforced isolation on the complete measurement channel.
- A functional-insulation converter such as Murata MEE1 is not an acceptable substitute for a safety-rated barrier in that supply path.
- The selected power-conversion path must preserve the protection level required by the final equipment standard and system insulation-coordination calculation.
- If six independent floating high-side supplies are retained, each supply crossing must independently satisfy the applicable barrier requirements, or a different formally assessed architecture must demonstrate equivalent protection.
- Do not join high-side grounds or bridge them to low-side ground through auxiliary rails, programming, debug, test equipment or communication interfaces.

## 3. Architecture gate

For each AMC1311B channel, retain distinct domains:
- High side: VDD1 and GND1, floating with respect to the low side and associated with the measured voltage potential.
- Low side: VDD2 and GND2, in the measurement electronics domain, subject to the final AGND/DGND bonding and EMC plan.
- Barrier: AMC1311B signal barrier plus the isolated supply path, PCB spacing, components, protective circuitry and any external connection paths.

The existing MASTER schematic audit found AMC1311B U3–U8 pins unconnected. This document does not imply the six devices are wired or that the intended architecture is implemented.

## 4. What remains unresolved

Before any converter is selected:
1. Freeze the TestStation product boundary and intended installation/connection point.
2. Confirm whether IEC 61010-1 and IEC 61010-2-030 are the applicable equipment standards, or whether a different system/product standard governs.
3. Derive required insulation class, working voltage, impulse/transient levels, measurement category, pollution degree and altitude.
4. Confirm whether the system needs reinforced insulation at every power and signal crossing.
5. Verify exact DC/DC converter certificate scope, continuous working voltage, impulse rating, creepage/clearance, protection and operating conditions.
6. Validate all secondary paths: USB/debug, Ethernet, shields, sensor returns, test connectors, programming headers and measurement interfaces.

The AMC1311B ratings are useful component evidence, not permission to set system requirements by copying the maximum device ratings.
