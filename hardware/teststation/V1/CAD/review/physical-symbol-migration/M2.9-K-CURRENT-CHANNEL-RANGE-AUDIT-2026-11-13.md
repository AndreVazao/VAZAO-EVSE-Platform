# M2.9-K — Current-channel range audit: TMCS1123 variant vs 32 A RMS requirement

Date: 2026-11-13  
Branch: `review/teststation-physical-symbol-migration`  
Status: **design discrepancy confirmed; no BOM or wiring change authorized**

## Executive finding

The historical TestStation requirement is 0–32 A RMS continuous per phase, with overload margin. The BOM currently names `TMCS1123A3AQDVGR` (75 mV/A). That variant's **linear measurement range is a peak instantaneous-current range**, not an RMS current rating.

For a sinusoidal 32 A RMS phase current:

`I_PEAK = 32 × √2 = 45.25 A`

The A3 variant's linear range is only ±32 A with a 5 V supply, and −32 A to +9.3 A with a 3.3 V supply. Therefore:
- at 5 V, it clips before reaching the 32 A RMS sinusoidal peak;
- at 3.3 V, it clips severely and asymmetrically;
- the family headline 80 A_RMS continuous input rating is a thermal/conductor capability, **not** the A3 variant's analog linear measurement range.

This is a material mismatch between the existing BOM choice and the documented 32 A RMS measurement requirement. The sensor's current-path thermal capability must not be confused with the output's linear measurement span.

## 2. Required peak range

For a sinusoidal current, `I_PEAK = I_RMS × √2`.

| Operating point | RMS current | Peak current | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Nominal | 32 A | 45.25 A | Existing continuous requirement |
| +25% overload | 40 A | 56.57 A | Preliminary engineering margin scenario |
| +50% overload | 48 A | 67.88 A | Higher transient/overload scenario |

The +25% and +50% values are analysis scenarios, not approved fault-current requirements. Real fault transients, crest factor, duration and protection coordination still need an explicit system requirement.

## 4. Sensor output vs ADS131M06 full-scale

TI gives the TMCS1123 transfer function:

`VOUT = (IIN × S) + VREF`

For A variants, the zero-current reference is 2.5 V; for B variants, it is 1.65 V. Pin 5 VREF is the precision internal zero-current reference output. It is not a generic external return and must not be confused with `*_I_RET`.

The ADS131M06 internal-reference differential full-scale at PGA gain 1 is ±1.2 V; higher PGA gains reduce that range. There is no gain setting below 1.

| Variant/scenario | Peak current | Sensor differential output magnitude (S × I_PEAK) | ADC PGA=1 ±1.2 V |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| A3, 32 A RMS | 45.25 A | 3.394 V | Exceeds ADC range; sensor itself already clips |
| A2, 32 A RMS | 45.25 A | 2.263 V | Exceeds ADC range |
| A1, 32 A RMS | 45.25 A | 1.131 V | Within range, only ~5.8% differential headroom |
| A1, 40 A RMS | 56.57 A | 1.414 V | Exceeds ADC range; requires attenuation/scaling |
| A1, 48 A RMS | 67.88 A | 1.697 V | Exceeds ADC range; requires attenuation/scaling |
| B1, 32 A RMS | 45.25 A | 1.131 V | Within range, only ~5.8% differential headroom |
| B1, 40 A RMS | 56.57 A | 1.414 V | Exceeds ADC range; requires attenuation/scaling |

This table compares differential signal magnitude with ADC differential FSR only. It does not prove pin common-mode compliance, input absolute maximum compliance, source impedance suitability, PGA stability, noise, filter response or analog front-end implementation.

The sensor's VOUT and VREF may be used as a differential pair in a design only after checking both pins' voltage ranges against ADS131M06 analog supply/common-mode constraints and ensuring adequate attenuation/headroom. A direct connection cannot be assumed safe simply because the differential voltage is within range.

TI ADS131M06 datasheet: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf  
PGA FSR: ±1.2 V / gain, using the nominal internal 1.2 V reference.

## 5. Critical correction to the 2026-11-13 interface evidence review

The earlier document `M2.9-K-TMCS1123-A3-ADS131M06-INTERFACE-EVIDENCE-REVIEW-2026-11-13.md` incorrectly stated that the A3 variant has no zero-current output voltage. This was a misreading of a vertically merged cell in the device-comparison table.

Correct TI facts:
- TMCS1123Axx: `VOUT,0A = VREF = 2.5 V`.
- TMCS1123Bxx: `VOUT,0A = VREF = 1.65 V`.
- TMCS1123Cxx: `VOUT,0A = VREF = 0.33 V`.
- Pin 5 VREF is the internal zero-current reference output; leave it floating only if not used.
- The A3 linear range remains ±32 A at 5 V and −32 A to +9.3 A at 3.3 V.

The earlier statement that A3 had no zero-current reference is withdrawn. The earlier caution not to connect VREF to the phase-return label remains correct: VREF is a reference output, not a current return.

## 6. Thermal rating is a separate constraint

TI lists maximum continuous input current of 80 A_RMS at 25 °C and 44 A_RMS at 125 °C in the electrical characteristics. TI also states the input-current safe operating area is constrained by junction temperature and derated at elevated ambient temperature.

Therefore:
- 80 A_RMS is not a universal PCB-level current rating under every ambient/thermal condition.
- 32 A_RMS nominal must be checked against actual ambient, copper geometry, package thermal path and enclosure.
- A 40 A_RMS overload scenario is below the tabulated 44 A_RMS at 125 °C by only 4 A; this does not establish a safe duration or thermal margin.
- Peak-current SOA and duration must be checked separately from analog linear range.

## 7. Existing 47 Ω / 4.7 nF note is not a completed filter design

M2.5 Section 6 mentions 47 Ω series resistance and a 4.7 nF differential capacitor, but it does not fully define:
- whether the resistor is per input or shared;
- exact connection to VOUT/VREF and ADC AINP/AINN;
- differential and common-mode transfer functions;
- loading of VREF/VOUT;
- ADS131M06 PGA-dependent input impedance;
- ADC modulator frequency and digital-filter configuration;
- required passband, stopband attenuation, alias rejection and phase matching;
- protection/clamp behavior and fault-current limits.

Do not populate or freeze 47 Ω / 4.7 nF based on that historical note. Recalculate and simulate the complete network after selecting the sensor variant and ADC gain/clock/filter configuration.

## 8. Required design decisions before net approval

1. Freeze actual maximum continuous RMS current per phase, not just the product headline 32 A.
2. Define overload current magnitude, waveform/crest factor, duration, repetition and protection response.
3. Select sensor variant based on peak linear range with margin, supply availability, output reference, ADC common-mode and thermal SOA.
4. Determine whether the sensor supply is 3.3 V or 5 V and how it is generated/distributed. Do not assume six isolated MEE1S2405SC converters feed current sensors; existing docs allocate those converters to AMC1311 voltage channels.
5. Design VOUT/VREF-to-ADS131M06 input scaling and filtering for the selected variant, including PGA=1 full-scale and common-mode constraints.
6. Decide whether ALERT/OC are used; calculate VOC threshold from the required current and document the hardware reaction. Do not connect OC/ALERT to safety functions without a qualified safety architecture.
7. Only after review, update the BOM/intent matrices and create a separate corrected review schematic.

## 9. Current decision

**A3 remains the existing BOM item but is now flagged NOT SUITABLE for the documented 32 A_RMS sinusoidal measurement requirement unless the requirement is changed or the design is otherwise materially reworked.**

A1 at 5 V and B1 at 3.3 V are comparison candidates only. A1 appears to offer the larger symmetric linear peak range; B1 aligns with the old 3.3 V sensor-supply note and a 1.65 V reference, but has less peak-range margin. Neither is selected.

No component value, footprint, symbol, pin-to-net destination, PCB, master schematic or legacy baseline was changed. PR #7 remains Draft and unmerged. No fabrication or mains energization is authorized.


## 10. Additional ADS131M06 input-voltage screening

TI's ADS131M06 recommended operating conditions specify, for PGA gains 1/2/4:
- AVDD: 2.7 V to 3.6 V;
- absolute AINxP/AINxN operating voltage: AGND − 1.3 V to AVDD;
- differential input range with internal reference: ±1.2 V / PGA gain.

This adds an important distinction: a signal can be inside the differential range and still violate the individual AIN pin voltage range.

### B1 at 3.3 V

For B1, VREF = 1.65 V and sensitivity = 25 mV/A:
- at 32 A_RMS sine: peak = 45.25 A; VOUT range approximately 0.519 V to 2.781 V; differential magnitude = 1.131 V. This is inside the ADC's ±1.2 V differential range, but with only about 5.8% differential headroom.
- at 40 A_RMS sine: peak = 56.57 A; VOUT range approximately 0.236 V to 3.064 V; differential magnitude = 1.414 V. The sensor remains inside its ±62 A linear range, and the pin voltages are inside a 3.3 V ADC rail, but the ADC differential FSR is exceeded. A differential attenuation factor of no more than approximately 0.849 is required just to fit ±1.2 V, before additional engineering margin.

This makes B1 at 3.3 V a promising architecture candidate if the actual required maximum instantaneous peak stays below ±62 A and the thermal SOA is met. It is not approved: a waveform crest factor above 1.414, transients, accuracy margin, ADC input loading, filter phase response and thermal derating can invalidate it.

### A1 at 5 V

For A1, VREF = 2.5 V and sensitivity = 25 mV/A:
- at 32 A_RMS sine: VOUT ranges approximately 1.369 V to 3.631 V.
- at 40 A_RMS sine: VOUT ranges approximately 1.086 V to 3.914 V.

With ADS131M06 AVDD = 3.3 V, the positive AIN pin would exceed the recommended AVDD input limit for both scenarios if VOUT is connected directly. A1 therefore requires a designed attenuation/level-shifting front end, not a direct VOUT/VREF connection. Its wider ±96 A sensor range is useful for peak headroom, but the analog interface is more involved.

### Design conclusion

B1 at 3.3 V currently appears the closer fit to the documented 3V3 measurement domain and ADC common-mode window, but it only covers ±62 A peak. A1 at 5 V has more sensor peak range but requires explicit analog level management before the 3.3 V ADC. Do not select either until the project specifies maximum instantaneous peak/crest factor, overload duration, ambient/board temperature and required ADC headroom.
