# M2.9-X — AMC1311B Isolation-Crossing Inventory and Review Matrix

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** REVIEW ONLY — NOT AN APPROVED INSULATION DESIGN  
**Scope:** Six AMC1311B voltage-measurement channels in TestStation V1.

## 1. Purpose

Record every presently identifiable galvanic crossing associated with the six AMC1311B channels, and define the evidence required before selecting isolated DC/DC converters or approving the measurement-domain architecture.

This document is an inventory and verification plan. It does not assign unverified nets, declare an insulation class, select components, or authorize construction or energization.

## 2. Evidence baseline

The current production MASTER, `hardware/teststation/V1/CAD/schematics/TS-V1-M2.9-K-MASTER.kicad_sch`, was audited with KiCad 10.0.8. The recorded audit found all eight pins of each AMC1311B instance U3–U8 unconnected in the exported netlist. Consequently, the present MASTER does not establish the high-side supply nets, low-side supply nets, sensor input nets, or a complete isolation-domain topology for these six channels.

The AMC1311B manufacturer datasheet (TI Rev C, June 2022) identifies separate high-side VDD1/GND1 and low-side VDD2/GND2 supply domains and an internal signal-isolation barrier. Published component barrier ratings are component-level data only; they are not a system insulation rating.

The floating high-side supply must cross from the low-side power domain. That DC/DC path is a second galvanic crossing in parallel with the AMC1311B signal barrier. It must not weaken the protection level required of the complete equipment.

## 4. Other possible barrier-bypass paths

The following paths must be traced from the actual schematic, PCB, harness and enclosure before the isolation architecture can be accepted:

| Potential path | Required review | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| DC/DC high-side supply | Working voltage, impulse, insulation class, certification conditions, creepage/clearance and fault behaviour | OPEN; safety-rated converter not selected |
| USB, programming and debug | Connector pinout, ground, shield, cable connection and whether accessible during operation | NOT MAPPED |
| Ethernet / communications | PHY isolation, magnetics, shield/chassis termination, PoE possibility and external cable exposure | NOT MAPPED |
| Sensor input and return wiring | Sensor-side reference, cable shield, connector pinout and connection to mains-referenced nodes | NOT MAPPED |
| Test and service connectors | Every pin, shell, shield and accessible test point | NOT MAPPED |
| Protective earth / chassis | Bonding topology and any intentional or parasitic connection to measurement domains | NOT MAPPED |
| Other power rails | Every regulator, auxiliary supply, USB power path and shared return | NOT MAPPED |
| PCB parasitic paths | Copper, mounting hardware, heatsinks, slots, contamination and creepage/clearance | NOT REVIEWED |

An internal signal-isolation component cannot compensate for a conductive bypass elsewhere in the product.

## 5. Architecture decisions still open

1. Define the product boundary and intended use: standalone measurement instrument, EVSE-integrated equipment, or factory/service fixture.
2. Establish the applicable safety-standard route and the measurement/transient environment before assigning insulation targets.
3. Define the six channel domains and whether high-side supplies are independent or grouped, with a documented fault-containment rationale.
4. Trace every galvanic path across the complete system, not only the AMC1311B signal barriers.
5. Select any DC/DC component only after continuous working voltage, transient/impulse, insulation classification, certification scope, altitude, pollution degree, material group, creepage and clearance are derived.
6. Verify startup, brownout, loss of one supply, shorted converter output, connector miswire and single-fault conditions.
7. Re-export the native KiCad netlist and perform a pin-to-net review after any approved schematic change.

## 6. Explicit exclusions

- The MEE1S2405SC is excluded from use as a safety-isolation element between a mains-referenced measurement domain and accessible SELV/control circuitry. Murata identifies the family as functional-insulation equipment and states it must not be used as an element of a safety-isolation system.
- A TLV70033 or other non-isolated regulator cannot create a galvanic isolation barrier.
- The AMC1311B component ratings must not be copied into a system-level insulation claim.
- A 1-second hi-pot figure alone is not evidence of continuous working-voltage capability or reinforced insulation.
- No converter MPN, shared high-side topology, net assignment, clearance, creepage, CAT rating or safety classification is approved here.

## 7. Acceptance evidence required

Before this matrix can be closed, the engineering record must contain:

- Approved product boundary, use case and applicable standards/editions.
- Complete six-channel pin-to-net map from the native KiCad MASTER.
- Domain diagram showing signal and power crossings, including all external interfaces.
- Component certificates and manufacturer conditions for every isolation component.
- Working-voltage, impulse, creepage and clearance calculations for the actual PCB and enclosure.
- Single-fault and fault-injection review.
- Independent qualified safety review.

## 8. Change boundary

This is a documentation-only review artefact. The production MASTER, legacy baseline, BOM, netlist, pin-to-net mapping, PCB, firmware and test procedures are unchanged. No fabrication, mains energization, overload test, conformity assessment or safety approval is authorized.
