## 3. Most relevant preliminary candidate — exact variant matters

RECOM's product documentation distinguishes the reinforced series suffixes and option codes. The preliminary candidate is **R24P05S/P/X2/R6.4** only as a datasheet-review target:

- 24 V nominal input and 5 V nominal output.
- 1 W, nominal 200 mA output.
- /P continuous short-circuit protection option.
- /X2 alternative pinout/clearance option; manufacturer describes greater than 9 mm input-output clearance for the X2 version.
- /R6.4 denotes 6.4 kVDC, 1-second isolation test option in the published ordering information.
- Manufacturer lists EN/IEC 61010-1 and other certifications for reinforced variants.

**Important:** A 6.4 kVDC/1-second test rating is not the continuous working voltage. RECOM's own isolation guidance distinguishes flash-test voltage from rated working voltage. Do not infer a system rating or approve this part from the 6.4 kV value. Exact variant certificate, conditions, working-voltage tables, altitude, pollution degree, creepage/clearance, PCB layout and system standard remain to be checked.

Also note the output is unregulated in the published candidate family. This may be acceptable only after high-side rail tolerance, load variation, ripple, sensor/AMC limits and ADC/measurement error are budgeted and verified.

## 4. Candidate selection checklist for R24P05S/P/X2/R6.4

Before it may be considered as a selected part, retrieve and review the current exact datasheet/certification package and record:

1. Exact MPN/order-code confirmation and pinout for /X2.
2. Rated continuous working voltage and conditions (AC/DC, repetitive peak, lifetime/altitude).
3. Reinforced/basic insulation classification and certificate scope applicable to the exact suffix.
4. EN/IEC 61010-1 certificate/CB report scope and conditions.
5. Impulse/transient ratings and test waveforms/conditions.
6. Component creepage/clearance values and any layout restrictions.
7. Output tolerance, load regulation, ripple, no-load and minimum-load behaviour.
8. Input range and surge/overvoltage limits against the actual 24 V rail.
9. Temperature derating and worst-case thermal assessment for six modules.
10. Isolation capacitance and common-mode transient/EMI behaviour.
11. Startup, shutdown, repeated start, short circuit and recovery behaviour.
12. Power budget: AMC high-side load plus sensor network/protection/other actual loads and margin.
13. Board keepouts, routing, pin escape and six-island separation.
14. Manufacturer confirmation/engineering support if any rating or certification condition is ambiguous.

A failed or unknown mandatory item rejects the candidate for the current design gate.

## 5. Six-channel cost/area implication (not a budget approval)

If six independent converters are ultimately required, multiply the selected converter's per-channel footprint, cost, input current and heat by six, while also accounting for six independent input/output crossings and six high-side island layouts. This is not equivalent to six independent barriers if external wiring or other circuitry reconnects the islands.

## 9. Exact manufacturer datasheet review — additional findings (2026-11-20)

The current RECOM manufacturer page links the family datasheet **RxxPxx/R, revision 6/2020**. The exact order-code sequence shown by the manufacturer is **R24P05S/R6.4/P/X2** (ordering examples), while its product page labels the same option combination **R24P05S/P/X2/R6.4**. Before purchasing or creating a footprint/BOM record, resolve the manufacturer ordering-code representation with RECOM/distributor and ensure the datasheet variant matches. Do not assume the ordering suffix sequence is interchangeable without confirmation.

### Datasheet values / limitations captured

- Input range: nominal input ±10%; for 24 V, nominally 21.6–26.4 V. The actual system source envelope and transients remain unverified.
- 5 V output: 200 mA nominal maximum (1 W); output accuracy ±5% max; typical load regulation 15% for 3.3/5 V outputs from 10–100% load; line regulation 1.2% per 1% Vin typical; ripple/noise 200 mVp-p at 20 MHz bandwidth.
- Minimum load is listed as 0%, but below 10% the datasheet says specifications may not be met. This matters because the real AMC1311B high-side load has not been calculated.
- Isolation test: 6.4 kVDC for 1 s; 3.2 kVAC/60 Hz for 1 minute for /R6.4. The datasheet still does not establish a continuous rated working-voltage value adequate for this system.
- Isolation table: reinforced insulation grade, 2MOPP at 34 Vrms, internal and external creepage/clearance listed >4.8 mm; /X2 product-page option advertises >9 mm input/output clearance. These values must be reconciled against the exact /X2 pinout, certificate and system insulation coordination; not treated as a system clearance approval.
- Isolation capacitance: 4–10 pF; leakage current stated <0.01 µA max; common-mode dv/dt qualification 65 kV/µs at 1 kV common-mode in datasheet feature section.
- Environment: -40 to +85 °C without derating according to the datasheet graph/context; maximum case temperature +105 °C; altitude 3000 m; pollution degree PD2. Six converters together still require thermal and layout evaluation.
- /X2 pinout is not the standard single-output pinout: pin 5 is no pin; pin 6 is -Vout; pin 7 is +Vout. Input pins 1 (+Vin) and 2 (-Vin). This exact footprint/pin map must be independently checked before any design use.
- EMC compliance statement references external filtering for EN55032 Class A/B; the module itself is not evidence of finished TestStation EMC compliance.
- Datasheet safety certifications list EN/IEC 61010-1:2010 / IEC 61010:2010, 3rd edition (report T1301251-313). The current applicable edition/amendments and certificate scope need checking against the project's standards gate.
- **Critical manufacturer caveat:** the datasheet says products are not authorized for safety-critical applications without RECOM's explicit written consent. A formal manufacturer confirmation for the intended TestStation use is therefore a mandatory gate before this candidate can be accepted, even if component ratings otherwise appear suitable.

### Updated decision

Datasheet review improves the evidence but does not close the working-voltage/system insulation requirement, exact order code/certificate, current standard edition, written manufacturer authorization for the intended safety-critical use, source transient envelope, high-side power budget, PCB spacing, thermal/EMC or fault analysis. ## 11. High-side supply load-budget check — AMC1311B (TI Rev C)

TI AMC1311B Rev C (June 2022) specifies VDD1 = 3.0–5.5 V for AMC1311B. High-side supply current with SHTDN low: 6.0 mA typical / 8.4 mA maximum at 3.0–3.6 V; at 4.5–5.5 V, 7.1 mA typical / 9.7 mA maximum. (AMC1311 non-B values differ; this note is specifically AMC1311B.)

At a nominal 5 V high-side rail, AMC1311B alone is therefore approximately 35.5 mW typical and 48.5 mW at the published 9.7 mA maximum. Across six channels, this is approximately 213 mW typical / 291 mW maximum, before any external divider, filter, protection, indicator or additional circuitry. This is a datasheet arithmetic estimate only, not a full rail budget.

**Critical interaction with RECOM unregulated modules:** the 1 W RxxPxx/R datasheet says output specifications may not be met below 10% load. For 1 W/5 V/200 mA, 10% load is 20 mA. AMC1311B high-side alone draws only 7.1–9.7 mA at 4.5–5.5 V, i.e. below this 20 mA threshold. The 2 W/5 V/400 mA candidate's 10% threshold would be 40 mA, even farther above the AMC-only load. Thus neither 1 W nor 2 W unregulated candidate can be assumed to meet rail tolerance/ripple at the actual load. Additional loading or a regulated topology might be needed, but do not add a dummy load or choose another module until total load, efficiency/thermal, output tolerance, startup/fault and measurement error implications are evaluated.

This is a **material candidate-screening concern** and strengthens the no-selection decision. Required next: calculate per-channel complete high-side load and evaluate actual output behavior across input/load/temperature, including minimum-load strategy and power wasted in any load resistor, before continuing shortlist.

TI source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/amc1311.pdf (AMC1311x Rev C, June 2022, section 7.9). RECOM source: RxxPxx/R datasheet revision 6/2020; link retained in document.
