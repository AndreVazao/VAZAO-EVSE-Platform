# M2.9-AB — Isolated DC/DC Candidate Screening (Evidence-Based, Not Selection)

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** PRELIMINARY DATASHEET SCREEN — NO COMPONENT APPROVED  
**Scope:** Potential 24 V auxiliary input / 5 V floating high-side rail for six AMC1311B channels  
**Previous gate:** M2.9-AA — requirements and selection gate

## 1. Why this screening exists

M2.9-AA established that exact system working voltage, transient/impulse environment, isolation objective, input source range, and high-side load remain OPEN. This preliminary screening compares publicly documented converter examples only to identify the evidence quality and avoid repeating the legacy MEE1S2405SC selection.

**Assumptions for search only, not frozen requirements:** 24 V nominal auxiliary input and 5 V nominal isolated output. The old 24 V system rail has not been verified for range, protection, ripple or fault conditions. AMC1311B high-side supply voltage/current requirements and total load budget must be checked against the exact selected device variant and datasheet before any candidate can pass.

## 3. Most relevant preliminary candidate — exact variant matters

RECOM's product documentation distinguishes the reinforced series suffixes and option codes. The preliminary candidate is **R24P05S/P/X2/R6.4** only as a datasheet-review target:

- 24 V nominal input and 5 V nominal output.
- 1 W, nominal 200 mA output.
- /P continuous short-circuit protection option.
- /X2 alternative pinout/clearance option; manufacturer describes greater than 9 mm input-output clearance for the X2 version.
- /R6.4 denotes 6.4 kVDC, 1-second isolation test option in the published ordering information.
- Manufacturer lists EN/IEC 61010-1 and other certifications for reinforced variants.

**Important:** A 6.4 kVDC/1-second test rating is not the continuous working voltage. RECOM's own isolation guidance distinguishes flash-test voltage from rated working voltage. Do not infer a system rating or approve this part from the 6.4 kV value. Exact variant certificate, conditions, working-voltage tables, altitude, pollution degree, creepage/clearance, PCB layout and system standard remain to be checked.

Also note the output is unregulated in the published candidate family. This may be acceptable only after high-side rail tolerance, load variation, ripple, sensor/AMC limits and ADC/measurement error are budgeted and verified.

## 4. Candidate selection checklist for R24P05S/P/X2/R6.4

Before it may be considered as a selected part, retrieve and review the current exact datasheet/certification package and record:

1. Exact MPN/order-code confirmation and pinout for /X2.
2. Rated continuous working voltage and conditions (AC/DC, repetitive peak, lifetime/altitude).
3. Reinforced/basic insulation classification and certificate scope applicable to the exact suffix.
4. EN/IEC 61010-1 certificate/CB report scope and conditions.
5. Impulse/transient ratings and test waveforms/conditions.
6. Component creepage/clearance values and any layout restrictions.
7. Output tolerance, load regulation, ripple, no-load and minimum-load behaviour.
8. Input range and surge/overvoltage limits against the actual 24 V rail.
9. Temperature derating and worst-case thermal assessment for six modules.
10. Isolation capacitance and common-mode transient/EMI behaviour.
11. Startup, shutdown, repeated start, short circuit and recovery behaviour.
12. Power budget: AMC high-side load plus sensor network/protection/other actual loads and margin.
13. Board keepouts, routing, pin escape and six-island separation.
14. Manufacturer confirmation/engineering support if any rating or certification condition is ambiguous.

A failed or unknown mandatory item rejects the candidate for the current design gate.

## 5. Six-channel cost/area implication (not a budget approval)

If six independent converters are ultimately required, multiply the selected converter's per-channel footprint, cost, input current and heat by six, while also accounting for six independent input/output crossings and six high-side island layouts. This is not equivalent to six independent barriers if external wiring or other circuitry reconnects the islands.

## 6. Preliminary conclusion

**No converter selected.** R24P05S/P/X2/R6.4 is retained only as the first candidate for exact datasheet/certificate review. This is not a design recommendation or safety approval. Candidate screening must be repeated or narrowed when the system requirements gate is closed. No component is authorized for BOM or schematic use.
