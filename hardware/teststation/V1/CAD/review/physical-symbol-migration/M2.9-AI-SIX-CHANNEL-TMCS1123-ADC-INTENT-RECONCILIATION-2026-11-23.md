## 1. Purpose and evidence rules

This matrix consolidates the source-declared current-channel grouping and records the unresolved pin-level decisions. It is not a wiring instruction and must not be used to modify MASTER.

Evidence states:
- **Source intent:** functional channel association written in project documents.
- **Manufacturer pin fact:** pin identity/function from the exact component datasheet.
- **Native netlist:** actual exported connectivity in the inspected MASTER netlist.
- **Approved connection:** requires a reviewed pin-to-net decision; none is granted by this matrix.

## 3. Source-document inconsistencies requiring explicit decisions

### 3.1 ADC differential polarity / net naming

M2.9-E assigns:
- ADC CH3: pin 3 AIN3N = `ZONE_x_L1_IP`; pin 4 AIN3P = `ZONE_x_L1_IN`.
- ADC CH4: pin 5 AIN4P = `ZONE_x_L2_IP`; pin 6 AIN4N = `ZONE_x_L2_IN`.
- ADC CH5: pin 7 AIN5N = `ZONE_x_L3_IP`; pin 8 AIN5P = `ZONE_x_L3_IN`.

Thus L1 and L3 labels place the `_IP` net on the N input and `_IN` on the P input, while L2 uses the opposite relationship. M2.9-J uses `Lx_I` / `Lx_I_RET` naming and does not define the physical polarity mapping. This must be resolved against the approved analog front-end transfer function and ADC channel configuration; do not normalize names or swap wires by assumption.

### 3.2 Sensor VREF and ADC negative input

TI identifies TMCS1123 pin 5 VREF as an **analog output** representing zero-current output reference. The datasheet says it may be left floating if unused. The source netlist assigns each sensor VREF to an `I_RET` net, but the source contracts do not explicitly prove that this net is the matching ADC negative input after the intended filtering/conditioning.

Decision required per channel: whether ADC P receives conditioned VOUT and ADC N receives conditioned VREF, or whether a different fully specified reference/return circuit is intended. Record filter values, impedance, common-mode range, polarity and error budget before approval.

### 3.3 Sensor supply rail

- M2.9-E: TMCS1123 pin 8 VS = `SENSOR_5V_x`.
- M2.9-J: VS = `3V3_MEAS`.
- M2.9-I: local 100 nF + 1 µF bypass baseline.

TI operating range is 3 V to 5.5 V, so the datasheet does not select between the two project choices. Select one canonical rail and define tolerance, source, six-device current budget, startup/transient behavior, ground reference and decoupling implementation.

### 3.4 Diagnostics and unused pins

Current TI TMCS1123 datasheet Rev. D (May 2026):
- Pin 4 ALERT: open-drain active-low; may connect to GND if unused.
- Pin 5 VREF: analog zero-current reference output; float if unused.
- Pin 7 OC: open-drain active-low; may connect to GND if unused.
- Pin 9 VOC: overcurrent threshold input; connect to VS if unused.
- Pin 10 NC: reserved; may be grounded or left floating.

The design must choose whether ALERT/OC are used, define pull-ups and MCU/safety destinations if used, set VOC threshold or connect it to VS if unused, and document NC treatment. No diagnostic/safety function is assumed implemented by the current MASTER.

Manufacturer source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf

## 7. ADS131M06 manufacturer pinout cross-check

TI's official ADS131M06 datasheet (SBAS949A, Rev. A) confirms the PBS 32-pin TQFP assignments:
- CH3: pin 3 = AIN3N; pin 4 = AIN3P.
- CH4: pin 5 = AIN4P; pin 6 = AIN4N.
- CH5: pin 7 = AIN5N; pin 8 = AIN5P.

Comparing these manufacturer facts with M2.9-E:
- CH3: pin 3 (AIN3N) is labelled `ZONE_x_L1_IP`, while pin 4 (AIN3P) is labelled `ZONE_x_L1_IN`.
- CH4: pin 5 (AIN4P) is labelled `ZONE_x_L2_IP`, while pin 6 (AIN4N) is labelled `ZONE_x_L2_IN`.
- CH5: pin 7 (AIN5N) is labelled `ZONE_x_L3_IP`, while pin 8 (AIN5P) is labelled `ZONE_x_L3_IN`.

Therefore the L1 and L3 prototype net labels are opposite to the P/N pin names, while L2 is aligned. This is a **source-contract naming/polarity inconsistency**, not proof that the physical sensor wiring must be reversed: the project must define what `IP` and `IN` mean electrically, including whether the intended signal polarity is deliberately inverted in firmware or analog conditioning. No net labels or pins are changed here.

## 9. Exact TMCS1123 variant versus nominal supply

The selected orderable part recorded in M2.9-E and M2.4 is `TMCS1123A3AQDVGR`. TI's current datasheet Rev. D explains that:
- TMCS1123 **AxA** variants use a 2.5 V zero-current output reference and are intended for bidirectional measurement with a 5 V supply.
- TMCS1123 **BxA** variants use a 1.65 V zero-current output reference and are intended for bidirectional measurement with a 3.3 V supply.
- The family operating supply range is 3 V to 5.5 V.

This makes the M2.9-E `SENSOR_5V_x` baseline consistent with the documented AxA variant intent, while M2.9-J's `3V3_MEAS` wording conflicts with the exact selected A3 variant's intended zero-current operating point. It does **not** prove that operation at 3.3 V is prohibited; it means the project must calculate output headroom, current range, ADC common-mode/differential limits, fault swing and calibration implications before choosing the rail. A 3.3 V rail cannot be accepted merely because it is inside the absolute operating range. Nor should the MPN be changed to a B variant without revalidating sensitivity, current range and procurement/BOM impact.

TI source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf (SBOSA24D, Rev. D, May 2026), Section 9.2.2; exact orderable part: https://www.ti.com/product/TMCS1123/part-details/TMCS1123A3AQDVGR.

Decision remains OPEN pending a quantitative sensor/ADC operating-point analysis and formal MPN/rail approval.


## 10. Corrected current-range check for the selected A3 sensitivity

The exact selected `TMCS1123A3AQDVGR` is the A3 sensitivity grade: 75 mV/A with 2.5 V zero-current output. TI TMCS1123 Rev. D, Table 4-1, gives the following output-swing-limited **linear measurement ranges**:
- VS = 5 V: −32 A to +32 A.
- VS = 3.3 V: −32 A to +9.3 A.

For a 32 A_RMS sinusoidal current:
`I_peak = 32 × √2 = 45.25 A`

Thus A3 cannot measure the 32 A_RMS sinusoid without clipping at either supply. The 80 A_RMS continuous thermal capability is a separate limit and must not be treated as the linear signal range. The TI table values supersede the earlier rough estimate derived from nominal output headroom.

For comparison, the same TI table lists:
- A1 (25 mV/A): ±96 A at 5 V; −96 A to +28 A at 3.3 V.
- A2 (50 mV/A): ±48 A at 5 V; −48 A to +14 A at 3.3 V.
- A3 (75 mV/A): ±32 A at 5 V; −32 A to +9.3 A at 3.3 V.

These are manufacturer linear ranges, not a final selection recommendation. A1 at 5 V is the only one of these three grades with comfortable sensor-side current headroom for 45.25 A peak; A2 at 5 V barely covers that peak (2.75 A nominal headroom), and A3 does not. The 3.3 V AxA operating ranges are strongly asymmetric and do not support the required bidirectional 32 A_RMS waveform.

**Disposition:** selected A3 sensitivity is a critical HOLD against a 32 A_RMS bidirectional requirement. The M2.4 statement that A3 supports bidirectional 32 A measurement is inconsistent with TI's linear range table. Do not silently replace the MPN: formally reopen the MPN freeze and assess current range, overload, crest factor, thermal/SOA limits, sensor accuracy, and ADC interface together.

## 11. ADS131M06 differential full-scale interaction

TI ADS131M06 Rev. A specifies differential full-scale range as ±1.2 V / PGA gain (internal 1.2 V reference):
- Gain 1: ±1.2 V
- Gain 2: ±600 mV
- Gain 4: ±300 mV
- Gain 8: ±150 mV
- Gain 16: ±75 mV
- Gain 32: ±37.5 mV
- Gain 64: ±18.75 mV
- Gain 128: ±9.375 mV

For a hypothetical A1 sensor (25 mV/A) at 32 A_RMS sinusoidal peak 45.25 A, the nominal sensor differential excursion relative to its zero-current reference would be:
`45.25 A × 0.025 V/A = 1.131 V peak`

That is inside the ADS131M06 ±1.2 V differential range only at PGA gain 1, with approximately 69 mV (5.75%) nominal headroom before sensor sensitivity tolerance, offset, waveform crest factor, overload, filter error and ADC reference/gain tolerances. Higher PGA gains would clip. This is a screening calculation only; it assumes the intended differential input is VOUT minus VREF and does not prove the input common-mode voltage, absolute pin voltage, ADC input network, or reference connection is valid.

A2 at the same peak would produce 2.263 V differential, above the ADC's ±1.2 V full scale even at gain 1, unless the analog front end deliberately attenuates or level-shifts it. A3's theoretical 3.394 V differential at 45.25 A is not reachable linearly because the sensor clips first, and it also exceeds ADC full scale.

**Mandatory coupled-design gate:** verify TMCS output linear range, ADS differential FSR/PGA, each ADC pin's common-mode and absolute voltage limits, VREF topology, filter attenuation/phase, input impedance, calibration and required overload margin. Do not infer that a sensor grade is compatible with the ADC merely because the sensor alone can measure the current.

Sources:
- TI TMCS1123 Rev. D, Table 4-1: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf
- TI ADS131M06 Rev. A, Section 8.3.3 / Table 8-1: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf

No sensor MPN, rail, PGA, schematic, netlist, BOM, PCB or firmware change is approved by this screening.


## 12. ADS131M06 absolute input-pin voltage constraint

TI ADS131M06 Rev. A, Recommended Operating Conditions Table 6-3, specifies each AINxP/AINxN absolute input voltage:
- PGA gain 1, 2 or 4: AGND − 1.3 V minimum to AVDD maximum.
- PGA gain 8, 16, 32, 64 or 128: AGND − 1.3 V minimum to AVDD − 1.8 V maximum.

The ADS131M06 AVDD itself is 2.7 V to 3.6 V. These absolute input limits are independent of the differential FSR. A differential value inside ±1.2 V does not make either input pin valid if its absolute voltage exceeds AVDD.

For the hypothetical direct A1/5 V sensor connection, assuming TMCS1123 VREF = 2.5 V and a 32 A_RMS sine:
- Peak current = 45.25 A.
- Sensor VOUT nominal positive peak = 2.5 V + (45.25 A × 25 mV/A) = 3.631 V.
- This exceeds ADS131M06 AVDD at a nominal 3.3 V supply and also exceeds the device's maximum AVDD of 3.6 V by about 31 mV.
- The negative peak is approximately 1.369 V.

Therefore, even the hypothetical A1 grade that has adequate sensor-side current range and nominal differential FSR at PGA=1 **cannot be assumed to connect directly** to the ADS131M06 using only the M2.4 47 Ω / 4.7 nF differential RC starting network. A designed attenuator/level-shift/buffer topology or a different approved interface may be required. Its effect on gain, noise, phase, common-mode and calibration must be calculated and verified.

This is a hard design gate: prove both (a) differential input voltage remains within the PGA FSR and (b) each AIN pin remains inside its absolute operating range for all current directions, overloads, tolerances and supply corners. No direct connection is approved.

Source: TI ADS131M06 Rev. A, Table 6-3 and Section 8.3.3: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf
