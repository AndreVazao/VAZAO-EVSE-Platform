## 2026-11-10 follow-up — U19 HV land-pattern coordinate reconstruction (review-only)

The TI Rev D DVG0010A example board-layout drawing (4226847/C, 10/2022; PDF page 46, zero-based index 45) was re-opened at full page resolution. The drawing itself supplies enough dimension relationships to construct a **candidate coordinate table** for the HV/isolation option without estimating pad locations from the package body:

- The HV option labels the row-centre separation as 9.75 mm. With the pattern origin at the midpoint between the two pad-row centre lines, the row centres are therefore X = −4.875 mm and X = +4.875 mm.
- The 8 signal pads on the right-hand row have 7 intervals × 1.27 mm pitch. The drawing marks this row SYMM about the horizontal centreline; the eight Y centres are therefore −4.445, −3.175, −1.905, −0.635, +0.635, +1.905, +3.175 and +4.445 mm.
- The two current pads on the left-hand row are symmetric about the horizontal centreline, with 5.08 mm centre-to-centre spacing; their Y centres are −2.540 and +2.540 mm.
- HV pad metal dimensions are 1.65 mm × 4.41 mm for the two current pads and 1.65 mm × 0.60 mm for the eight signal pads, with R0.05 typical corners as shown. With X along the row-to-row axis and Y along the pad rows, the dimensions are recorded as X × Y.
- This yields a nominal metal edge-to-edge X gap of 9.75 − 1.65 = 8.10 mm, consistent with TI's explicit “8.1 mm clearance/creepage” label. The calculation is an internal cross-check of the drawing interpretation, not an independent insulation test.

**Candidate numbering/orientation transcription:** use the TI top-view numbering shown on the package drawing: pins 1 and 2 are the two left-side current pads (upper then lower in the TI drawing); pins 3 through 10 occupy the right-side signal row from lower to upper. The CSV uses X negative for the current-pad row, X positive for the signal-pad row, and Y positive toward the upper edge of the TI land-pattern drawing. A KiCad footprint may use a different local axis convention; any conversion must be explicit and checked in the footprint editor against the TI top view.
