## U2 — ADS131M06IPBSR

Official TI datasheet:
- URL: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf
- Document: SBAS949A, Rev. A, February 2021.
- Orderable addendum (PDF page 98): ADS131M06IPBSR is Active Production, TQFP (PBS), 32 pins.
- Pin configuration (PDF page 4) and pin-function table (PDF page 5) identify the 32 physical TQFP pins. Relevant serial pins: CS=17, SCLK=19, DOUT=20.
- Datasheet front matter states TQFP body size 5.00 mm × 5.00 mm (nominal). The TI PBS mechanical drawing (PDF page 99; drawing PBS S-PQFP-G32, 4087735/B, 07/05) specifies 5.05/4.95 mm body, 7.10/6.90 mm lead span, 32 leads at 0.50 mm pitch, lead width 0.23/0.17 mm, and lead length 0.70/0.40 mm.
- TI package materials: https://www.ti.com/ods/sysadd/pm/symlink/ads131m06_pm.pdf — PBS, TQFP, 32 pins; reel carrier dimensions are not PCB land-pattern dimensions.

**Correction to the previous register:** the PBS/TQFP drawing was present in the datasheet near the end (PDF page 99); the previous statement that it was absent was incorrect. The PBS land-pattern data (PDF page 100; drawing 4212229/A, 10/11) specifies an example 6.20 mm × 6.20 mm pattern, 28 × 0.50 mm pitch callout, and pad geometry 1.60 mm × 0.30 mm. The exact drawing must be visually checked before translating all callouts into pad-centre coordinates; do not confuse this with the RSN/WQFN drawing.

KiCad candidate inspected: `Package_QFP:LQFP-32_5x5mm_P0.5mm` (official KiCad footprint library; candidate only). Its 32 pads and 0.50 mm pitch/body 5 × 5 mm family are geometrically relevant, but its inspected pad geometry is 1.20 mm × 0.28 mm with pad-row centres at ±3.25 mm. TI's PBS example calls for 1.60 mm × 0.30 mm pads and a 6.20 mm pattern callout. Therefore this stock footprint is **not an exact match** to the TI example and is NOT APPROVED. `TQFP-32_7x7mm_P0.8mm` is explicitly the wrong pitch and must not be used.

## U19 — TMCS1123A3AQDVGR

Official TI datasheet:
- URL: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf
- Document: SBOSA24D, Rev. D, July 2023, revised May 2026.
- Package information identifies DVG (SOIC, 10 pins); package size is nominal 10.3 mm × 10.3 mm including pins.
- Pin configuration/function table (PDF page 4): pin 1 IN+, 2 IN−, 3 GND, 4 ALERT, 5 VREF, 6 VOUT, 7 OC, 8 VS, 9 VOC, 10 NC.
- Current accessible TI datasheet Rev. C (SBOSA24C, revised January 2025): mechanical package outline DVG0010A is on PDF page 36; example board layout / land pattern is on PDF page 37. The drawing calls out the 10-pin arrangement and includes both an IPC-7351 nominal option and an HV/isolation option. The two patterns are not interchangeable without reviewing the isolation requirements.
- TI package-materials page identifies the exact A3AQDVGR orderable variant as DVG, SOIC, 10 pins. It is logistics data, not PCB land-pattern approval.

**Important layout constraint:** TI's land-pattern drawing explicitly presents an HV/isolation option with 8.1 mm clearance/creepage and an IPC-7351 nominal option with 7.3 mm clearance/creepage. The datasheet insulation section specifies at least 8 mm external clearance and creepage. The PCB footprint choice and surrounding copper/board geometry therefore require an insulation review; selecting a generic SOIC-10W footprint solely by pad count is not acceptable.

The DVG outline specifies 7 × 1.27 mm pitch per row, nominal body width 7.5 mm (7.6/7.4), package span 10.3 mm nominal including pins, and 10 pads. The TI land-pattern example uses 10 pads 1.65 mm long in the HV/isolation option, 1.27 mm pitch, and an overall row separation callout of 5.08 mm; the IPC nominal alternative uses 10 pads 2.00 mm long and a different row separation. The stock KiCad library index inspected did not establish an exact 10-pin DVG footprint. No generic SOIC-10 footprint is approved.
