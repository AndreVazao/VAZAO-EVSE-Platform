# TestStation V1 — A1 current-channel analogue front-end study

Date: 2026-11-13  
Status: **ENGINEERING STUDY — NO CIRCUIT, BOM OR PIN-TO-NET APPROVAL**  
Branch: `review/teststation-current-afe-design-study`  
Scope: one channel topology, to be replicated and checked for all six TMCS1123 channels.

## 1. Purpose and design boundary

This study translates the provisional current envelope into an ADC-compatible signal-chain target. It does not create a production schematic and does not authorize component placement, fabrication, overload testing or mains energization.

The provisional envelope remains:
- 32 A RMS continuous per phase;
- ±70 A instantaneous measurement target;
- 40 A RMS / 10 s and 48 A RMS / 1 s as analysis-only scenarios;
- ADS131M06 PGA = 1 as the initial ADC setting.

The TMCS1123A1AQDVGR at 5 V remains the preferred **study candidate only**. The existing A3 BOM entry is not changed.

## 2. Manufacturer facts used

### TMCS1123A1

TI TMCS1123 Rev. D (SBOSA24D, revised May 2026) states:
- A1 sensitivity nominally 25 mV/A;
- A variants use a 2.5 V zero-current reference;
- sensor supply range is 3.0 V to 5.5 V;
- maximum continuous input current is listed as 80 A RMS at 25 °C and 44 A RMS at 125 °C, subject to the datasheet thermal conditions;
- maximum VOUT capacitive load is 4.7 nF;
- analogue bandwidth is 250 kHz;
- the device requires 100 nF local VS-to-GND decoupling close to the pins.

The 80 A RMS figure is not a guarantee for this PCB, enclosure or ambient temperature. The overload scenarios remain prohibited from physical testing until thermal and protection analysis is complete.

### ADS131M06

TI ADS131M06 Rev. A states:
- AVDD recommended operating range 2.7 V to 3.6 V;
- with PGA gain 1/2/4, each AIN pin's recommended range is AGND − 1.3 V to AVDD;
- internal-reference differential full-scale is ±1.2 V / PGA gain;
- the PGA input impedance at gains 1, 2 and 4 is approximately 330 kΩ × 4.096 MHz / fMOD (nominal relationship), so source impedance and the actual clock/modulator configuration must be included in gain-error analysis.

Differential full-scale and individual AIN pin voltage are separate constraints. Both must pass.

## 3. Signal arithmetic for A1

Nominal transfer function:

`VOUT = VREF + IIN × S`

For the A1 candidate:
- VREF = 2.5 V nominal;
- S = 0.025 V/A nominal;
- at +70 A: VOUT = 4.25 V;
- at −70 A: VOUT = 0.75 V;
- sensor differential signal relative to VREF = ±1.75 V.

The ±70 A target is within the A1 nominal ±96 A linear sensor range. It does not prove thermal safety or transient recovery.

The datasheet sensitivity error is up to ±0.4% under its stated conditions. A simple sensitivity-only high-side screening value is therefore 25.1 mV/A. This is not a complete worst-case error budget; temperature drift, offset, reference, lifetime, amplifier, ADC and resistor errors remain to be combined.

## 4. ADC transfer target — revised screening value

The earlier 0.60 attenuation was only a first-pass arithmetic suggestion. It leaves only 150 mV nominal differential headroom at ±70 A against a ±1.2 V ADC full-scale, before combining tolerances and transient overshoot. It is therefore **not frozen and is superseded for this study**.

Use a nominal differential transfer factor of **0.50 or lower as the next design-study target**, subject to full error-budget analysis:

`VADC_DIFF = 0.50 × (VOUT − VREF)`

At ±70 A:
- nominal differential magnitude = 0.875 V;
- sensitivity-only +0.4% screening magnitude = 0.8785 V;
- remaining margin to nominal ±1.2 V FSR = 321.5 mV (26.8% of FSR).

With an output common-mode target of 1.65 V, an ideal balanced differential driver would produce:
- AINP = 1.65 V + VADC_DIFF/2;
- AINN = 1.65 V − VADC_DIFF/2;
- at ±70 A, the two pins span approximately 1.21075 V to 2.08925 V using the sensitivity-only high-side screening value.

These are transfer targets, not resistor values or a released circuit. The actual design must include ADC reference tolerance, amplifier gain/offset, resistor ratio tolerance and drift, common-mode accuracy, transient headroom, and sensor sensitivity/offset over temperature and lifetime. The 0.50 factor may need to be reduced further.

## 5. Candidate signal-chain architecture

Proposed functional chain for each current channel:

1. TMCS1123A1 at a regulated 5 V sensor rail, with local 100 nF decoupling and rail-noise review.
2. High-input-impedance sensing of VOUT and VREF as a differential measurement pair. Do not treat VREF as a phase return or load it directly with a low-impedance divider.
3. A precision differential conditioning stage that subtracts VREF from VOUT, scales the signal and provides a defined interface to a fully differential ADC driver.
4. A fully differential driver/filter stage with output common-mode controlled near 1.65 V.
5. A symmetric, ADC-compatible differential RC/active filter.
6. ADS131M06 differential input, initially PGA=1.

The exact amplifier topology and part are intentionally not selected. The A1 signal's individual VOUT voltage reaches 4.25 V at +70 A, so a 3.3 V-only input stage cannot be assumed to tolerate it. The selected input stage must explicitly support the sensor-side common-mode/input range across supply and temperature, while the output stage must meet the ADC's 3.3 V domain and output common-mode requirements.

A single fully differential amplifier may or may not meet the required input common-mode and source-impedance conditions. A buffered subtractor followed by an FDA is also a candidate. Compare both using actual manufacturer models and worst-case data before choosing.

## 6. VREF loading and sensor-output constraints

The TMCS1123 VREF pin is a precision zero-current reference output. It is not a generic ground, current return or supply. The circuit must:
- measure VOUT relative to VREF without materially loading either output;
- verify the datasheet's output drive/load limitations for the exact orderable;
- avoid exceeding the 4.7 nF maximum capacitive load specified for VOUT;
- assess VREF noise, VOUT noise, sensor supply coupling and reference correlation;
- provide defined behaviour if VREF or VS is missing or outside tolerance.

Do not connect VREF directly to the ADC AIN pin or a passive network until loading, voltage range and common-mode have been proven.

## 7. Filter and phase requirements

Do not reuse the historical 47 Ω / 4.7 nF note as a completed filter. The final network must be calculated from:
- ADS131M06 clock, modulator frequency, data rate and digital-filter configuration;
- desired measurement bandwidth for 50/60 Hz current, harmonics and transient diagnostics;
- out-of-band attenuation and alias rejection;
- ADC input impedance and driver stability;
- differential and common-mode transfer functions;
- channel-to-channel component matching and phase error;
- noise density and integrated input-referred noise;
- overload recovery and saturation behaviour.

All six channels must use a controlled matched network. Component tolerance and temperature coefficient must be included in phase/gain mismatch analysis.

## 8. Six-channel scaling and calibration

The architecture must be reviewed as six simultaneous channels, not just one isolated channel:
- six A1 sensor supply currents and decoupling transients;
- 5 V rail load and distribution impedance;
- reference-buffer loading and cross-channel coupling;
- six driver/filter channels and thermal dissipation;
- common-mode and ground-return layout;
- ADC channel mapping and differential polarity;
- per-channel zero-current offset and gain calibration;
- temperature drift and calibration retention;
- over-range detection, invalid-sample flags and recovery.

No ADC channel pair, polarity, VOUT/VREF pin destination or schematic net is approved by this document.

## 9. Required verification before schematic implementation

1. Extract all relevant TMCS1123A1 electrical limits, including VREF/VOUT load conditions and temperature/lifetime errors.
2. Select candidate precision amplifier/FDA parts whose input common-mode, output swing, noise, offset, bandwidth, slew rate, stability and supply range all pass.
3. Build a worst-case DC error/headroom spreadsheet, including sensor sensitivity/offset, reference, resistor ratios, amplifier and ADC reference.
4. Calculate the ADS131M06 PGA input impedance at the selected clock and modulator rate; verify driver settling and gain error.
5. Design and simulate differential/common-mode filters, including channel matching and phase error at 50/60 Hz and required harmonics.
6. Simulate startup, sensor-rail failure, VREF failure, ADC rail failure, input over-range, output saturation and recovery.
7. Review six-channel power, thermal and layout constraints.
8. Complete thermal SOA and conductor/terminal/PCB analysis before any overload test.
9. Only then produce a separate review-only schematic and pin-to-net intent table.
