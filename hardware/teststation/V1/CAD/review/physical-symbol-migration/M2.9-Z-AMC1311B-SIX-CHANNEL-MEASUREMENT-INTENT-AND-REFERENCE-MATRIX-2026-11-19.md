## 1. Purpose

The native production MASTER currently does not establish the pin-to-net connections for AMC1311B U3–U8. Therefore, neither their exact measurement targets nor their reference/domain relationships can be inferred reliably from the schematic evidence reviewed.

This matrix captures the information that must be established before power-domain grouping, sensor wiring, converter selection, pin mapping, or circuit implementation. Unknown values are explicitly marked **OPEN / NOT ESTABLISHED**. No channel assignment is inferred.

## 3. Requirements that must be recorded for every channel

| Requirement | Evidence / value needed | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Measurement purpose | Voltage/current/other, target quantity | Determines sensor and channel function |
| Measurement nodes | Exact terminal/node names and polarity | Prevents ambiguous or unsafe wiring |
| Nominal range | Expected operating range and sign | Sensor scaling and ADC design |
| Maximum continuous voltage | Value and operating condition | Working-voltage and insulation assessment |
| Transient / impulse envelope | Source, waveform, magnitude, duration, repetition | Insulation coordination and protection |
| Measurement environment | Installation context and external-circuit category if applicable | Applicable standard and protection requirements |
| Sensor architecture | Direct divider, transducer, isolated sensor, or other; currently undecided | Reference, barrier and interface analysis |
| Reference / return | Exact connection and whether shared | Determines conductive coupling between channels |
| Common-mode conditions | Expected and fault common-mode values | Sensor/AFE limits and isolation requirements |
| Channel-to-channel isolation | Required withstand/protection or none, justified | Determines whether grouping is allowed |
| Fault containment | What must remain safe/functional if channel fails | Power-island and protection design |
| User/access interfaces | Connectors, shields, test points, cables | Finds bypasses and accessible hazards |
| Accuracy/bandwidth | Required error, bandwidth, phase and timing | AFE/ADC/filter choices |
| Overload behaviour | Expected overloads, recovery, fault detection | Protection and system robustness |

## 4. Reference-compatibility review

Before two or more channels may share a high-side power island, the review must explicitly establish:

- Their intended sensor/input wiring does not create an unsafe or unwanted conductive path.
- Their voltage/reference relationship is compatible under normal operation and relevant faults.
- Common-mode voltages stay within the selected sensor and AMC1311B limits.
- Shared return does not invalidate measurement accuracy, channel isolation or fault-containment requirements.
- External cables, shields, connector shells, service interfaces and other circuitry do not create hidden paths.
- The required system-level insulation protection is maintained across every crossing.
- The grouping decision has a documented review and acceptance record.

A blank or unknown answer means **grouping is not justified**; it does not automatically mean a specific alternative architecture is approved.

## 5. Evidence sources and capture rules

Use, in order of evidence strength:
1. Approved product requirements and intended-use statement.
2. Verified wiring/connection specification and system interface documents.
3. Native KiCad schematic/netlist after review.
4. Manufacturer datasheets and component certification records.
5. Measurements from an approved, safe test setup and documented test procedure.

Do not fill this matrix using coordinate guesses, labels alone, assumed phase assignments, old unverified drawings, or inferred channel roles. Reconcile every recorded channel assignment against the native MASTER and the approved product requirements.

## 6. Gate before architecture or implementation

This matrix is ready for review as a capture checklist only. It is **not complete** and does not approve a power-domain architecture.

The next design gate requires:
- Product boundary and intended use.
- A verified definition of U3–U8 measurement purpose and input nodes.
- Channel reference/return relationships.
- Voltage and transient environment per channel.
- Required channel-to-channel isolation and fault containment.
- Standards-derived insulation and interface requirements.
- Qualified review before any schematic/BOM/netlist/PCB implementation.

## 7. Change boundary

Documentation-only. No production MASTER, BOM, netlist, pin-to-net mapping, PCB, firmware or test procedure changed. No channel roles, nets, power grouping, converter MPN, CAT rating, insulation class, clearance, creepage or safety claim approved. No fabrication or energization authorized.
