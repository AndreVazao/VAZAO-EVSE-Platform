## 1. Purpose and evidence distinction

The native production MASTER does not establish actual pin-to-net connectivity for AMC1311B U3–U8: the KiCad-exported netlist audit found their pins unconnected. However, repository source documents do contain legacy/source-declared functional intent. This matrix records that intent without treating it as implemented or approved connectivity.

Use the following evidence states distinctly:
- **Source-declared intent:** functional grouping appears in legacy notes or a documented wiring contract.
- **Native connectivity:** exported from actual KiCad wires/labels/pins in the production MASTER.
- **Verified pin mapping:** physical pin identity checked against the exact component manufacturer datasheet/package.
- **Approved design intent:** reviewed and accepted requirements, including safety/interface conditions.

The legacy intent is useful for requirements recovery but is not sufficient to authorize schematic wiring or power-domain decisions.

## 4. Reference-compatibility review

Before two or more channels may share a high-side power island, the review must explicitly establish:

- Their intended sensor/input wiring does not create an unsafe or unwanted conductive path.
- Their voltage/reference relationship is compatible under normal operation and relevant faults.
- Common-mode voltages stay within the selected sensor and AMC1311B limits.
- Shared return does not invalidate measurement accuracy, channel isolation or fault-containment requirements.
- External cables, shields, connector shells, service interfaces and other circuitry do not create hidden paths.
- The required system-level insulation protection is maintained across every crossing.
- The grouping decision has a documented review and acceptance record.

The legacy source intent currently specifies six dedicated measurement supply domains. That is evidence of an earlier architecture intention, not a substitute for a current system safety review. Do not consolidate those domains without a documented technical review. Conversely, do not assume the old MEE1 converter selection is acceptable for the required safety-isolation role.

A blank or unknown answer means **grouping is not justified**; it does not automatically mean a specific alternative architecture is approved.

## 5. Evidence sources and capture rules

Use, in order of evidence strength:
1. Approved product requirements and intended-use statement.
2. Verified wiring/connection specification and system interface documents.
3. Native KiCad schematic/netlist after review.
4. Manufacturer datasheets and component certification records.
5. Measurements from an approved, safe test setup and documented test procedure.

Source-declared mappings may be recorded as historical/legacy intent, but must not be promoted to “connected,” “verified,” or “approved” without further evidence. Reconcile every channel assignment against the native MASTER and approved product requirements.
