# M2.9-U — MEE1S2405SC manufacturer insulation classification decision

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** COMPONENT EXCLUDED FROM SAFETY-ISOLATION BARRIER — DOCUMENTED MANUFACTURER LIMITATION  
**Parent:** M2.9-S isolated-supply requirements; M2.9-T standards applicability  
**Scope:** Exact part MEE1S2405SC, Murata MEE1 Series, 24 V input / 5 V output, SIP package.

## 1. Primary manufacturer evidence

The exact Murata product page identifies MEE1S2405SC as:
- 24 V nominal input, continuous range 21.6–26.4 V;
- 5 V, 200 mA, 1 W output;
- 1,000 VDC isolation test figure;
- Safety approval: UL60950.

Official product entry: https://pim.murata.com/en-us/pim/details/?partNum=MEE1S2405SC

Official MEE1 series datasheet (downloaded from the Murata product page; current product page points to this PDF):
https://pim.murata.com/asset/pim4/isolatedDCDCconverter/KDC_MEE1_PDF_ISOLATEDDCDCCONVERTER?lastModifiedDatetime=20260908184059

The datasheet's technical note titled “ISOLATION VOLTAGE” explicitly states:
- The 1 kVDC value is a production flash/hi-pot test for 1 second.
- The MEE1 is recognised by UL to UL 60950 for **functional insulation**.
- Both input and output should normally be maintained within SELV limits (less than 42.4 V peak or 60 VDC).
- The isolation test voltage is a measure of immunity to transient voltages.
- The part “should never be used as an element of a safety isolation system.”
- Any further barrier between these circuits and user-accessible circuitry must be provided by additional insulation systems in accordance with the applicable safety standard.

The datasheet's “SAFETY APPROVAL” section identifies UL file E151252 and states the 24 V input models are recognised at maximum ambient temperature 85 °C. The datasheet also states the MEE1 series is not internally fused and specifies an anti-surge input fuse for UL 60950 recognition; the 24 V family fuse recommendation is 0.16 A, subject to confirming the exact application and applicable approval conditions.

## 2. Engineering decision

**MEE1S2405SC is excluded from the safety-isolation barrier between a mains-referenced measurement domain and an accessible SELV/control domain.**

This is not an inference from the 1 kV test voltage. It follows directly from Murata's explicit functional-insulation classification and prohibition against using the part as an element of a safety-isolation system.

The previous M2.9-S HOLD is therefore resolved for this specific intended safety-barrier role: it is a documented exclusion, not merely missing evidence. The part may still be suitable for functional noise isolation or other applications where both sides remain within SELV limits and the applicable system design permits it; that is outside this decision and does not authorize its use in this TestStation barrier.

## 3. Architecture implications

1. Do not use the six existing MEE1S2405SC instances as the six floating AMC1311B high-side safety barriers.
2. Do not repurpose these modules as isolated supplies for TMCS1123 channels if those channels are mains-referenced and the supply barrier is part of the personnel-protection boundary.
3. Keep the AMC1311B high-side/low-side supply domains physically and electrically separate in the architecture; each high-side domain requires a converter/isolator whose exact safety certification and working-voltage ratings satisfy the eventual system insulation-coordination requirements.
4. The low-side 5 V supply candidate for TMCS1123/THP210 remains a separate rail decision. A common low-side rail is not itself a safety barrier.
5. Reassess any proposed use of MEE1 in non-safety functional isolation only after its placement, SELV status, faults, EMC and thermal loading are documented.

## 4. Remaining evidence required for replacement converter

Do not select a replacement based on hi-pot voltage alone. For each candidate exact MPN and package, collect:
- safety certification and exact insulation classification (basic/reinforced as required);
- continuous working voltage rating across the barrier;
- repetitive peak and transient/impulse ratings;
- certified creepage and clearance;
- pollution degree, material group/CTI and altitude conditions;
- certificate/file number and exact family/suffix coverage;
- input fuse, fault, thermal and output loading conditions;
- isolation capacitance/common-mode behaviour for measurement accuracy and EMC.

The system-level insulation requirement remains open until product boundary, measurement category, terminal exposure, transient environment, pollution degree, altitude and protective measures are fixed under the applicable product standard.

## 5. Design boundary

Review documentation only. No MASTER schematic, BOM, netlist, pin mapping, PCB, firmware or test procedure has been changed. No replacement MPN selected. No fabrication, mains energization, hazardous testing, certification or safety approval is implied.
