## 3. Important contract conflicts

### 3.1 Isolated AMC1311B supply

M2.6 §17 documents:
`24V -> MEE1S2405SC -> +5V_ISO-x -> TLV70033DCKR -> +3V3_ISO-x -> AMC1311B`

M2.9-I §4 instead describes:
`24V_SELV -> MEE1S2405SC -> ISO_5V_x / ISO_GND_x -> AMC1311B`

These are not interchangeable descriptions. Before any connection is drawn, verify the exact AMC1311B VDD1/VDD2 operating ranges and which side each rail is intended to power, then decide whether the TLV70033 is required, where it belongs, and whether both sides need separate supply treatment. The current MASTER contains no TLV70033 instance and no AMC1311B pin connections.

### 3.2 Current sensor supply

M2.9-J §6 states VS -> 3V3_MEAS. M2.9-M shows that for symmetric ±70 A linear measurement, the compared A1A candidate requires 5 V; its 3.3 V linear range is asymmetric. The existing MASTER still contains A3 symbols and no connected VS net. Do not implement the old 3.3 V note or substitute A1A until the rail, FDA and thermal constraints are reviewed together.

### 3.3 Converter reference mapping

M2.9-J maps U9/U10/U11 to A1/A2/A3 and U12/U13/U14 to B1/B2/B3. The MASTER netlist resolves four ISO5 labels on VIN+ pins of U10/U11/U13/U14 instead. U9/U12 have no connected pins; ISO5_A3/B3 labels are dangling. This mismatch must be corrected in a reviewed mapping table, not by blindly moving labels or swapping component references.

## 5. Reconstruction sequence

1. **Freeze baseline:** preserve the KiCad-generated MASTER netlist, ERC and 145-row register in M2.9-P.
2. **Resolve source power:** identify the actual 24 V input source/connector, fuse/protection, input return, operating range and fault envelope. The current label alone is not a source.
3. **Resolve isolated voltage channels:** create a six-row converter-to-channel table; verify MEE1S2405SC input/output pin numbers, isolation domains, output capacitance, load and thermal conditions. Do not share isolated returns.
4. **Resolve AMC1311B rails:** use TI datasheet VDD1/VDD2 pin requirements and choose 5 V/3.3 V distribution per side; reconcile M2.6 and M2.9-I.
5. **Resolve ADC power/reference:** select and document ADC reference, AVDD/DVDD, AGND/DGND and clock domains. Confirm the actual symbol represents every required power pin.
6. **Resolve current sensor:** select variant and rail only after confirming the ±70 A linear requirement, THP210 topology, output common-mode, FDA input limits, ADC range and thermal SOA.
7. **Resolve ground ownership:** specify AGND_A/B, DGND_A/B, sensor ground, converter floating returns, MCU ground, CAN isolation and chassis/PE boundaries. Identify any intentional single-point connection.
8. **Resolve channel mapping:** approve the six sensor-to-ADC channel assignments and all signal/diagnostic paths, then remove the three U19/U2 cross-nets.
9. **Implement review-only schematic changes:** new branch/commit, no MASTER production promotion. Export KiCad netlist/ERC after each controlled change and compare against M2.9-P baseline.
10. **Independent review:** require no unexplained ERC errors, verified pin-to-net matrix, power budget, sequencing/fault analysis and review of all isolation boundaries before any production decision.

### 3.1.1 AMC1311B isolation-barrier supply constraint — manufacturer clarification

TI's AMC1311 datasheet Rev C identifies two electrically separate supply domains:
- VDD1/GND1 = high-side supply and ground (pins 1 and 4).
- VDD2/GND2 = low-side supply and ground (pins 8 and 5).
- For AMC1311B, VDD1-to-GND1 is 3.0–5.5 V and VDD2-to-GND2 is 3.0–5.5 V; TI's typical condition is 5 V high-side and 3.3 V low-side.
- TI's simplified application diagram explicitly shows separate high-side and low-side supplies and grounds on opposite sides of the reinforced isolation barrier.

Source: TI AMC1311x datasheet Rev C, Sections 3, 5, 6 and 7.3: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/amc1311.pdf

**Isolation warning:** an LDO such as TLV70033 powered from an isolated converter output shares that converter output's return. It cannot be used to power the opposite AMC1311B side unless a separate isolated domain is provided; otherwise tying both sides to the same return would bypass the amplifier's isolation barrier. M2.6's `+5V_ISO-x -> TLV70033 -> +3V3_ISO-x` drawing does not identify which rail feeds VDD1/GND1 and which feeds VDD2/GND2. M2.9-I's direct `ISO_5V_x / ISO_GND_x -> AMC1311B` statement is also incomplete because it does not specify both supply domains.

This is now a **critical architecture HOLD**. The intended low-side supply may be the common measurement rail `3V3_MEAS`, but the current documents do not explicitly establish that pin mapping, and the MASTER netlist has all U3–U8 pins unconnected. Do not implement the M2.6 LDO chain as written until the two sides, two returns, isolation barrier and ADC-side supply ownership are explicitly drawn and reviewed.
