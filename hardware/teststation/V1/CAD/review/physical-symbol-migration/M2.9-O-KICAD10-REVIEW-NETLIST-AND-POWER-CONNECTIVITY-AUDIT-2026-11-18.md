## 1. Executive result

The native KiCad 10.0.8 export has superseded the earlier coordinate-only connectivity interpretation in M2.9-N. The generated netlist is authoritative for the exported review schematic and shows:

1. On all six migrated TMCS1123 symbols U15–U20, the functional labels `*_I` are attached to physical pin 8 (VS), and `*_I_RET` labels are attached to physical pin 9 (VOC). The actual signal pins VREF (5) and VOUT (6), GND (3), IN+ (1) and IN− (2) are unconnected.
2. On the six MEE1S2405SC symbols U9–U14, the labels `ISO5_A1`, `ISO5_A2`, `ISO5_B1`, `ISO5_B2` appear on pin 1 (VIN+) of U10/U11/U13/U14 respectively. All converter VIN− pins and all VOUT−/VOUT+ pins are unconnected. U9 and U12 have all four pins unconnected.
3. The seven inspected power/reference pins on each ADS131M06 U1/U2 are unconnected in this review-migration export.
4. KiCad ERC for the review migration export reports 383 errors and 53 warnings, compared with 300 errors and 53 warnings in its review baseline: +83 errors. This is not an ERC pass.

These results identify incorrect/incomplete connectivity in the exported review-migration schematic. They must not be silently mapped onto the separate native MASTER until the dedicated MASTER workflow produces and uploads its own KiCad netlist.

## 3. TMCS1123 connectivity resolved by KiCad

All six migrated symbols have the same pin-to-net pattern:

| Physical pin | TI function | KiCad-resolved net |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | IN+ | Unconnected |
| 2 | IN− | Unconnected |
| 3 | GND | Unconnected |
| 4 | ALERT | Unconnected |
| 5 | VREF | Unconnected |
| 6 | VOUT | Unconnected |
| 7 | OC | Unconnected |
| 8 | VS | `A_L1_I`, `A_L2_I`, `A_L3_I`, `B_L1_I`, `B_L2_I`, `B_L3_I` respectively |
| 9 | VOC | corresponding `*_I_RET` label |
| 10 | NC | Unconnected |

**Critical implication:** the current labels intended to represent the current-sensor signal pair are not connected to VOUT/VREF in this exported migrated schematic. They are connected to VS/VOC. This is a symbol-to-wire/pin mapping defect in the review artifact and must be resolved before using this migration as a basis for analog-front-end design. Do not fix by simply swapping labels: first verify symbol pin coordinates, orientation, datasheet pinout and intended THP210 topology, then run regression checks and a new KiCad netlist.

## 10. Baseline-to-migration netlist delta — U19 / ADC B

The review baseline netlist contains three suspicious cross-device nets:
- `Net-(U19-GND)`: U19 pin 3 (GND) tied to U2 pin 17 (CS).
- `Net-(U19-IN-)`: U19 pin 2 (IN−) tied to U2 pin 20 (DOUT).
- `Net-(U19-VS)`: U19 pin 8 (VS) tied to U2 pin 19 (SCLK).

These three exact net pairings are absent from the migrated review netlist; U19 pins 2/3/8 and U2 pins 17/19/20 are unconnected there. This removes the known legacy cross-device shorts from the migrated export, but it does not make the migrated channel correct: the six current labels have instead landed on TMCS pin 8 VS and pin 9 VOC, while VREF/VOUT and the current-path pins remain unconnected. Treat the two observations separately: the U19-to-U2 legacy shorts were removed, but the replacement signal mapping is still wrong/incomplete.
