## U19 coordinate-frame correction

The coordinate CSV uses TI drawing coordinates with Y positive toward the top of the drawing. KiCad footprint-local Y positive points down the screen. The correct transform is:

- `X_KiCad = X_TI`
- `Y_KiCad = -Y_TI`

The candidate footprint and `validate_kicad_footprint_load.py` implement this transform. The native validator also checks:

- pin 1 above pin 2;
- pin 3 below pin 10;
- current-input row left of signal row;
- 9.75 mm row-centre separation;
- 8.89 mm signal-row vertical span.
