# TestStation V1 — TMCS1123 variant, sensor rail and ADC-rate screening

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Status:** ENGINEERING SCREENING — NOT A BOM OR CIRCUIT APPROVAL  
**Branch:** `review/teststation-current-afe-design-study`  
**Related:** PR #8 stacked on PR #7  
**Scope:** reconcile the ±70 A peak current-measurement target with current TMCS1123 datasheet limits, existing sensor supply contracts and ADS131M06 data-rate options.

## 1. Executive conclusions

1. For a **bidirectional ±70 A linear measurement range**, the current TI TMCS1123 Rev D comparison table supports **TMCS1123A1A at 5 V** (linear range ±96 A). This remains a study candidate, not a selected BOM item.
2. TMCS1123A1A at 3.3 V is **not** a bidirectional ±70 A solution: its datasheet linear range is −96 A to +28 A. The asymmetry is caused by the 2.5 V zero-current output and reduced positive output headroom.
3. TMCS1123B1A at 3.3 V is symmetric but limited to ±62 A, so it does not reach the ±70 A measurement target. B1A at 5 V is also asymmetric (−62 A to +130 A), and therefore does not meet a symmetric ±70 A target.
4. Existing 5 V isolated supplies in M2.9-I are explicitly assigned to the six AMC1311B voltage channels. They are not a defined common 5 V supply for TMCS1123 or THP210. A separate, correctly referenced 5 V analog rail and its load/sequence/noise budget are not yet documented.
5. The TMCS1123A1A ±96 A figure is an **analog linear range**, not a thermal-current approval. The sensor SOA must independently be checked against 32 A RMS continuous, ambient temperature, PCB copper/airflow, and the unspecified duration/duty of the ±70 A peak event.
6. ADS131M06 data-rate options can be screened from TI's datasheet if an 8.192 MHz HR-mode clock is selected as a *candidate*: OSR 1024/512/256/128 gives 4/8/16/32 kSPS. No rate is selected until required harmonic bandwidth, phase error, latency, noise and SPI budget are specified.

Table 4-1 gives the following linear measurement ranges:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| A1A | 25 mV/A | 2.50 V | ±96 A | −96 A to +28 A | 5 V: YES linear; 3.3 V: NO |

The ranges are limited by output swing to supply/ground, not by thermal limits. TI explicitly requires checking both the continuous DC/RMS and transient peak current SOA.

### Implication

The existing M2.9-J instruction `VS -> 3V3_MEAS` cannot be retained unchanged if the design proceeds with A1A and requires symmetric ±70 A. It is not sufficient to change the net label: the power tree, regulator, sensor output domain, THP210 input network, power sequencing and protection must be reviewed together.

A1A at 5 V is the only symmetric ±70 A option among the compared A/B variants in this table. This is a **datasheet screening conclusion only**, not approval to power or connect the sensor.

## 4. Thermal SOA is a separate approval gate

TI Rev D Section 7.3 states that input-current SOA depends on self-heating, leadframe heating/fusing, current amplitude and duration, ambient temperature, PCB construction and airflow. The SOA curves are based on the TMCS1123EVM or an equivalent PCB with no airflow.

Relevant datasheet points:
- Electrical table: approximately 80 A_RMS continuous at 25°C and 44 A_RMS at 125°C (EVM conditions).
- Figure 7-4: continuous RMS capability falls as ambient temperature rises; about 82 A at 25°C and about 43 A at 125°C.
- Repetitive pulsed events must satisfy both pulsed SOA and continuous RMS constraints.
- TI's pulsed SOA depends on pulse duration, duty cycle and ambient temperature; single-event capability is a separate curve and must not be generalized to repetitive events.

Project interpretation:
- 32 A_RMS continuous remains the current project measurement requirement.
- ±70 A peak remains a provisional measurement envelope, not an approved overload withstand rating.
- For a continuous 50/60 Hz sine wave, 70 A peak would equal 49.5 A_RMS and must not be assumed thermally acceptable at high ambient temperature.
- The intended duration, repetition and duty cycle of the ±70 A peak event remain unspecified and are a hard HOLD.

The ±96 A linear output range must never be presented as a 96 A thermal current rating.

## 5. Sensor output range at ±70 A

For A1A at 5 V:
- Sensitivity nominal: 25 mV/A.
- Zero-current output: 2.5 V.
- At ±70 A nominal: 2.5 V ± 1.75 V = 0.75–4.25 V.
- With the previous +0.4% sensitivity-only screening: signal magnitude = 1.757 V, giving 0.743–4.257 V.

The sensitivity-only screen is not a complete worst-case error budget. It excludes zero-current offset, thermal drift, lifetime drift, nonlinearity, supply effects, reference loading, resistor ratios, FDA errors and ADC reference/gain errors.

The sensor output is above the 3.3 V domain at the positive extreme. A1A therefore needs a 5 V sensor supply if the symmetric ±70 A range is retained, followed by a properly protected and attenuated interface into the 3.3 V ADC domain.

## 6. ADS131M06 data-rate screening

Source: TI ADS131M06 datasheet SBAS949A, Sections 8.3.5 and 8.3.7:  
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf

TI states:
- CLKIN may be external or generated by a crystal/oscillator network.
- For best performance, the modulator sampling clock should be synchronous with SCLK; TI recommends supplying CLKIN and making data retrieval synchronous to it.
- (f_{MOD}=f_{CLKIN}/2) in the stated mode.
- (OSR=f_{MOD}/f_{DATA}), configured through CLOCK.OSR[2:0].
- OSR changes data rate, digital-filter bandwidth and noise.

If 8.192 MHz CLKIN and high-resolution (HR) mode are used as a **comparison basis only**:

| OSR | Output data rate | Samples per 50 Hz cycle | Samples per 60 Hz cycle |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1024 | 4 kSPS | 80 | 66.7 |
| 512 | 8 kSPS | 160 | 133.3 |
| 256 | 16 kSPS | 320 | 266.7 |
| 128 | 32 kSPS | 640 | 533.3 |

These are manufacturer-supported clock/OSR combinations, not project settings. The actual clock implementation remains HOLD in M2.9-J. The 8.192 MHz clock must not be assumed to exist on the board.

### Selection criteria before choosing OSR

1. Required measurement bandwidth and highest harmonic to report with a defined amplitude/phase error.
2. 50 Hz and 60 Hz performance, including frequency deviation and non-integer-cycle windows.
3. Total latency and transient/event capture requirements.
4. ADC noise at the selected OSR and PGA gain.
5. Digital filter amplitude/phase response and group delay.
6. SPI frame length, CRC/status overhead, two-ADC readout timing and STM32G474 CPU/DMA budget.
7. THP210 output settling and ADS131M06 input impedance at the selected fMOD.
8. Analog anti-alias network response and channel-to-channel matching.

The TI 1 kΩ-per-leg / 10 nF differential reference network remains a topology to evaluate, not a frozen filter. Do not use its approximate 7.96 kHz pole as proof of adequate anti-alias performance without the selected clock, OSR and required signal bandwidth.

## 7. THP210 interface checks retained

TI THP210 Rev C datasheet:  
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/thp210.pdf

- Absolute maximum differential voltage between IN+ and IN−: ±0.5 V. Any greater differential must be current-limited to 10 mA or less.
- Input common-mode operating range: VS− + 1 V to VS+ − 1 V.
- On a 5 V single supply, 1–4 V is the input common-mode interval.
- The A1A VOUT/VREF pair common-mode estimate of 1.625–3.375 V lies inside that interval as a first-pass DC screen.
- This does not prove closed-loop pin differential, stability, output swing, fault current, startup or powered-off compatibility.

A complete closed-loop network simulation remains mandatory before selecting THP210 or resistor values.
