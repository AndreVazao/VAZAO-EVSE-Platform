## 3. Consolidated blockers

### B-01 — U19/U2 cross-domain topology mismatch

The read-only geometry, exported-netlist and functional-contract reviews establish that three direct joins in the present MASTER conflict with the documented U19-B2 current-sensing function:

- U19 pin 3 GND to U2 pin 17 CS.
- U19 pin 2 IN− to U2 pin 20 DOUT.
- U19 pin 8 VS to U2 pin 19 SCLK.

The exact corrected circuit and physical pad mapping are still HOLD. Do not repair by changing labels, pin electrical types, ERC exclusions or wiring from this document.

**Closure evidence:** approved pin-to-net intent for exact order codes; manufacturer pin table; symbol and physical footprint pad mapping; independent review; a separate native KiCad candidate schematic; fresh netlist showing the approved topology and no unintended cross-domain joins.

### B-02 — Six TMCS1123 current-sensor paths are not complete

M2.9-AH reports that VOUT is unconnected on U15–U20 and that the current sensor power/reference/interface paths are not represented as a complete circuit in the exported MASTER. The current-channel functional intent has been recovered but remains source-intent, not approved pin-to-net implementation.

Additional gates from M2.9-AH / M2.9-AI:
- Exact order code and package: TMCS1123A3AQDVGR, 10-pin DVG; the current master symbol is not a valid production mapping.
- Current envelope ambiguity: the historical 32 A wording does not consistently distinguish peak from RMS. The provisional 70 A peak target is recorded in M2.9-AI but requires approval against the product requirement.
- A3 / 75 mV/A is HOLD against 32 A RMS sinusoidal measurement (45.25 A peak) and against the provisional ±70 A peak envelope.
- A2 / 50 mV/A at 5 V is only ±48 A linear range; it does not cover the ±70 A peak target.
- A1 / 25 mV/A at 5 V is a candidate for further interface analysis only; sensor-side linear range does not establish ADC compatibility.

**Closure evidence:** approved peak/RMS/continuous/overload waveform envelope; exact sensor MPN/rail; pin-5 VREF role; pin-6 VOUT filtering and polarity; ALERT/OC/VOC treatment; analogue error and phase budget; thermal/overload recovery; calibration plan; independent pin-to-net review.

### B-03 — ADC differential and absolute input limits are not closed

M2.9-AI records the ADS131M06 differential full-scale limit of ±1.2 V at PGA gain 1 (reduced with higher gain). It also records that individual AIN absolute-voltage and common-mode limits must be checked separately. A sensor output that is within its own linear range can still exceed the ADC differential range or pin limits.

**Closure evidence:** exact ADC variant/package; physical pin map; chosen reference/bias and gain; all operating and fault corners; individual AIN absolute voltage; common-mode; differential FSR; tolerance, transient, filter, phase and loading calculations. Direct TMCS1123-to-ADS131M06 connection is not approved by the existing screening.

### B-04 — U3–U8 AMC1311B voltage-measurement paths are not implemented

The current MASTER/netlist evidence recorded in M2.9-AE / M2.9-AF shows U3–U8 disconnected; no complete high-side input, supply, low-side supply or output path is established. Source-declared channel intent in M2.9-Z is not a connected circuit.

**Closure evidence:** exact AMC1311B order code and pin mapping; input-node and polarity requirements per channel; divider/protection/loading design; high-side and low-side supply; output conditioning/ADC mapping; common-mode and isolation-domain analysis; channel accuracy/bandwidth/phase budget; review of all six channels individually.

### B-05 — Legacy isolated-converter architecture is not acceptable as a safety barrier

The legacy assignment MEE1S2405SC is not approved as a safety-isolation element for mains-referenced measurement. M2.9-X, M2.9-Z and M2.9-AA/AB/AC require a replacement architecture and a system-derived insulation design. The existing candidate comparisons do not select a converter or approve grouping.

**Closure evidence:** product boundary, intended connection/category, working and transient/impulse voltage, insulation objective, pollution degree, material group, altitude, accessible interfaces, fault containment, required certification/conditions, per-channel load and six-channel aggregate budget; exact converter certificate/manual; qualified review. Six independent domains remain a candidate, not an approved topology.

### B-06 — Physical package/pad mapping is incomplete

Review-only candidates exist for ADS131M06IPBSR and TMCS1123A3AQDVGR. They are not assigned to the MASTER or PCB-A. PCB-A remains placeholder geometry and cannot establish pad connectivity or clearance.

**Closure evidence:** exact MPN/package; manufacturer drawing; independent pad-number and coordinate review; footprint rendering/load validation; complete PCB isolation/creepage/clearance review; assigned reviewed footprint in a separate candidate after schematic approval.

### B-07 — ADC polarity and reference naming conflict

M2.9-AH / M2.9-AI identify source-label polarity inconsistencies for ADS131M06 AIN3/AIN5 relative to TI physical pin names. L2 is aligned in the cited source mapping; L1/L3 labels appear reversed relative to physical P/N naming. The intended signal sign and firmware convention remain unresolved. TMCS1123 VREF is an output; its destination must be explicitly designed, not inferred from a name.

**Closure evidence:** approved semantic definitions of positive/negative current; exact ADC physical pin table; sensor VREF/OUT topology; approved net-intent matrix; firmware sign convention and calibration test.

## 5. Safe reconstruction sequence

1. **Freeze evidence identity:** re-fetch main, MASTER blob, legacy source, native schematic, netlist and ERC artifact; record hashes/commit and tool versions.
2. **Requirements closure:** resolve current envelope and measurement node/environment assumptions before selecting sensor/AFE or insulation.
3. **Pin/package verification:** verify exact physical pins and symbol electrical types for ADC, current sensors, voltage amplifiers, MCU and converter candidates. Review-only footprints remain unapproved.
4. **Interface design review:** complete current and voltage channels, reference/bias, filters, gain, protection, common-mode, absolute input limits, isolation and calibration budgets.
5. **Approve pin-to-net intent:** one row per physical pin, exact MPN, signal meaning, domain, destination and source evidence; independent review.
6. **Create a separate candidate schematic through KiCad native editor/import/save workflow:** do not edit the production MASTER as part of this gate and do not fabricate .kicad_sch S-expressions manually.
7. **Verification:** export fresh netlist; compare against approved pin-to-net matrix; run ERC; classify each finding by root cause; no blanket suppression. Confirm the three U19/U2 suspicious joins are absent only if the approved design says they should be absent, and confirm all six current/voltage channels are mapped as approved.
8. **PCB gate:** only after schematic/pin map approval, assign reviewed footprints and run DRC, including system insulation constraints. Placeholder PCB-A is not evidence.
9. **Qualified safety review:** review isolation, accessible interfaces, protection, fault behavior and applicable standards before any physical build or energisation.

## 6. Required automated and review checks for a candidate

The candidate reconstruction is not accepted by file presence or green CI alone. It must include:

- Exact-source and exact-tool provenance.
- Symbol/package/pad audit with manufacturer sources.
- Approved expected-net assertions for every physical pin.
- Domain-crossing and unintended-connection checks.
- Complete six-channel inventory and missing-pin/rail checks.
- ERC report with root-cause classification; unresolved errors and warnings remain visible.
- Review of NC treatment and power-flag strategy.
- Separate PCB DRC after schematic approval.
- Independent technical review and safety approval gates.
