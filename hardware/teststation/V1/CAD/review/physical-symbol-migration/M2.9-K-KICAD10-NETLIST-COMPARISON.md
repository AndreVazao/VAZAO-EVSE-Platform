## Netlist findings

The schematic text outside `lib_symbols` was preserved byte-for-byte, but the electrical netlist was **not** preserved. Replacing embedded symbol definitions changed the physical pin anchor positions relative to existing wires and labels.

| Physical device pin | Baseline connectivity | Migrated connectivity |
|---|---|---|
| U19 pin 3 GND | `Net-(U19-GND)`, shared with U2 pin 17 CS | Unconnected |
| U2 pin 17 CS | `Net-(U19-GND)`, shared with U19 pin 3 GND | Unconnected |
| U19 pin 2 IN− | `Net-(U19-IN-)`, shared with U2 pin 20 DOUT | Unconnected |
| U2 pin 20 DOUT | `Net-(U19-IN-)`, shared with U19 pin 2 IN− | Unconnected |
| U19 pin 8 VS | `Net-(U19-VS)`, shared with U2 pin 19 SCLK | `/B_L2_I` |
| U2 pin 19 SCLK | `Net-(U19-VS)`, shared with U19 pin 8 VS | Unconnected |
| U19 pin 9 VOC | Not represented as a physical pin in the old symbol | `/B_L2_I_RET` |

The migrated pin 9 VOC landing on `/B_L2_I_RET` is an observed geometric connection only; it is **not** approval of the intended circuit function or net assignment.

## Interpretation and required next work

1. Keep this PR isolated and do not merge it into production.
2. Perform a separate, datasheet-backed audit of each symbol pin anchor coordinate against the exact package/footprint pad numbering.
3. Reposition or redraw the review-copy wiring only after pin-anchor geometry is verified. Do not infer replacement destinations from existing net names.
4. Re-export netlists and ERC after each controlled change; compare physical pin number to net, not only unchanged schematic text.
5. Review all affected devices, not only U19/U2, before considering any production migration.

The production master and legacy baseline remain untouched. Footprint-pad mapping, intended net destinations, E-stop energy-removal/contactor-feedback design, and REF1 implementation remain unresolved. No fabrication or mains energization is authorized by this report.
