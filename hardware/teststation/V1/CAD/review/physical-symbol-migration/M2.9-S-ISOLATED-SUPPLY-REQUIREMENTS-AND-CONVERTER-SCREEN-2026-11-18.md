# TestStation V1 — isolated-supply requirements and converter screening

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** REQUIREMENTS / COMPONENT-SCREENING HOLD — REVIEW ONLY  
**Parent evidence:** M2.9-P, M2.9-Q and M2.9-R  
**Production decision:** none. No converter, rail, pin-to-net mapping or insulation class is approved.

## 1. Purpose and present decision

M2.9-P established from the native KiCad 10.0.8 MASTER netlist that the current power tree is not implemented: U9–U14 converter outputs are open, U3–U8 AMC1311B pins are all open, and the TMCS1123 channels have unresolved supply/ground/output connections. This document converts the architecture discussion into explicit procurement and design requirements. It does not repair the schematic.

**Decision:** retain six independent high-side supply domains as the reference architecture for further analysis, but do not retain MEE1S2405SC as an approved safety component. Keep one common low-voltage measurement domain as the simplest candidate for the ADC side, subject to ground/EMC review. Create a separate low-side 5V_MEAS rail for the TMCS1123A1A/THP210 study only if that AFE is retained. No part number is selected.

## 2. AMC1311B supply requirements

TI AMC1311x datasheet Rev C identifies two electrically distinct supply domains:

| Domain | Pins | Recommended supply | Reference |
|---|---|---:|---|
| High side | VDD1 pin 1, GND1 pin 4 | 3.0–5.5 V for AMC1311B | Floating with the sensed voltage |
| Low side | VDD2 pin 8, GND2 pin 5 | 3.0–5.5 V | ADC / low-voltage measurement ground |

TI's typical condition is 5 V on VDD1 and 3.3 V on VDD2. The two returns must not be joined. The isolation barrier is the functional safety boundary, not merely a signal-conditioning feature.

Manufacturer references: [TI AMC1311x datasheet, Rev C](https://www.ti.com/lit/gpn/AMC1311) and [product page](https://www.ti.com/product/AMC1311).

**Consequence:** one isolated 5 V converter output may supply VDD1/GND1. It cannot then feed a TLV70033 whose output supplies VDD2 while both regulator input and output returns remain connected. An LDO regulates voltage; it does not create galvanic isolation. The opposite-side supply must come from the low-side domain or from a second genuinely isolated output whose isolation ratings and grounding are explicitly designed.

## 3. Reference power-domain topology (candidate only)

    Protected 24V_SELV distribution (source and protection TBD)
      +-- isolated DC/DC HS-A1 --> 5V_HS_A1/GND_HS_A1 --> AMC-A1 VDD1/GND1
      +-- isolated DC/DC HS-A2 --> 5V_HS_A2/GND_HS_A2 --> AMC-A2 VDD1/GND1
      +-- ... six independent floating high-side outputs ...
      +-- isolated DC/DC HS-B3 --> 5V_HS_B3/GND_HS_B3 --> AMC-B3 VDD1/GND1

    Each AMC1311B isolation barrier separates its high-side pair
    from its low-side VDD2/GND2 pair.

    3V3_MEAS low-side domain --> all AMC VDD2/GND2 (subject to A/B
    ground-domain, ADC-reference and EMC decision)

    24V_SELV --> low-side regulator --> 5V_MEAS
                                      +--> TMCS1123A1A x6
                                      +--> THP210 x6
                                           (if this AFE is retained)

Rules:
1. Each high-side output/return is a floating pair. Never join GND_HS_x to AGND/DGND or another phase's high-side return.
2. Each AMC low-side supply pair is referenced to the selected low-side measurement ground. Confirm whether AGND_A/B and DGND_A/B are one domain or separate domains before joining them.
3. Six isolated converter inputs may share a protected 24V_SELV bus only after verifying the converters' input-to-output isolation, working-voltage and transient ratings for the maximum common-mode stress of their outputs.
4. A common 3V3_MEAS supply for AMC low sides is a candidate, not an instruction to short existing AGND_A/AGND_B nets. ADC reference, analog return, digital return, EMC currents and physical partition must determine the final grounding strategy.
5. 5V_MEAS is a separate low-side rail. It is not one of the six floating high-side rails and must not be derived from an AMC high-side return.

## 4. MEE1S2405SC — screening outcome

Murata product data identifies MEE1S2405SC as a nominal 24 V input, 5 V / 200 mA / 1 W isolated DC/DC converter. The published product isolation figure is 1000 V. Manufacturer references: [Murata MEE1S2405SC product entry](https://pim.murata.com/en-global/pim/details/?partNum=MEE1S2405SC) and [Murata datasheet download](https://www.murata.com/en-global/api/pdfdownloadapi?cate=cgsubPowerIsoDcDc&partno=MEE1S2405SC).

The 1000 V figure must not be treated as a continuous working-voltage rating or proof of reinforced insulation. The evidence reviewed for this study does not establish all values needed to approve it in this application: VIORM/VIOWM, repetitive peak working voltage, surge/VIOTM, insulation class (basic/reinforced), pollution degree, material group, creepage/clearance on the component and PCB, altitude, certification scope, and conditions of the manufacturer's safety recognition.

**Screening result: NOT APPROVED for any mains-referenced floating measurement domain.** This is not a declaration that the converter is inherently unsafe; it means the evidence available here is insufficient to demonstrate suitability for the actual 230/400 VAC system. Do not infer suitability from a one-minute dielectric test or a marketing isolation figure.

Required before reconsideration:
- Current manufacturer datasheet and safety approval/certificate file for the exact suffix and production revision.
- Manufacturer-confirmed continuous working voltage and surge rating, including whether the rating applies input-to-output and under which pollution/altitude/material conditions.
- Insulation coordination calculation for actual phase-to-earth and phase-to-phase maximum stress, overvoltage category, pollution degree, altitude and applicable equipment standard.
- PCB creepage/clearance, slots, component placement, connector and test-point review across the complete barrier.
- Thermal, input surge, short-circuit, output regulation and failure-mode analysis.

If any required rating is absent or below the calculated requirement, screen a different converter with explicit safety approvals and working-voltage data. Do not select a replacement by nominal isolation-test voltage alone.

## 5. 24V_SELV input bus requirements

The native MASTER currently does not show a verified, connected 24V_SELV source/distribution tree. Before choosing six DC/DC modules, define:
- Source origin: external SELV supply or internal AC/DC stage, including exact isolation class and approvals.
- Input operating range, tolerance, ripple, startup ramp, hold-up and brownout limits.
- Maximum input surge/transient and reverse-polarity conditions.
- Branch protection per converter or justified grouped protection; fuse coordination and fault-current interruption.
- Connector, cable, PCB copper, return path and thermal current ratings.
- Whether one converter failure can pull down the bus and affect all channels.
- Protective-earth/chassis relationship and separation between SELV and measurement domains.

Do not treat the label 24V_SELV as proof that the upstream source is actually SELV or that every downstream floating output is safe for mains common-mode voltage.

## 6. Load screening — low-side 5V_MEAS

For the A1A-at-5V AFE candidate:

| Load | Quantity | Current basis | Aggregate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| TMCS1123 | 6 | 14 mA maximum each | 84 mA |
| THP210 | 6 | 0.95 mA typical each | 5.7 mA typical |

Sensor-only load is 0.42 W at 5 V. The 5.7 mA FDA figure is typical, not a guaranteed maximum. This is **not** a regulator rating or complete load budget. It excludes FDA output loading, ADC/reference and other 5 V loads, regulator efficiency/quiescent current, startup charging, tolerance, temperature, fault conditions and design margin.

A low-side 24V-to-5V regulator should be screened against the complete worst-case budget, transient response, ripple/noise spectrum, conducted coupling into the six sensors, THP210 stability/PSRR, ADC sampling interaction, thermal dissipation and power sequencing. The switching converter should not be selected until the required analogue noise floor and channel-to-channel phase error are quantified.

## 7. Power-up, brownout and single-fault cases

| Scenario | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| 24V source absent / slow ramp | No undefined or back-powered rail; outputs remain safe |
| One high-side DC/DC open/short | Only intended channel affected; no cross-feed |
| High-side present, low-side absent | AMC outputs and ADC pins within absolute maximum ratings |
| Low-side present, high-side absent | Missing-supply behaviour understood; no signal-path back-power |
| 3V3_MEAS brownout / ADC reset | Defined output state, reset and recovery; no false valid measurement |
| 5V_MEAS short/open | Predictable sensor/FDA failure; no rail collapse or latch-up |
| LDO short/open | Failure remains within one galvanic domain; no barrier bypass |
| Converter insulation failure | Fault propagation, protective measures and safe state analysed |
| One phase transient | Other channels and low-voltage interfaces within common-mode/transient limits |
| Power-down sequencing | No damaging partial-power state or ADC input overdrive |

The final matrix must use actual component datasheets, MCU/ADC absolute maximum values, protection components and fault-clearing assumptions. It cannot be closed from a block diagram alone.
