**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** ENGINEERING DOCUMENTATION / POWER-ARCHITECTURE HOLD  
**Branch:** `review/teststation-current-afe-design-study`  
**Related:** PR #8 stacked on PR #7  
**Scope:** reconcile M2.6, M2.9-I, M2.9-J and M2.9-M before any current-sensor supply or analogue-front-end implementation change.

## 1. Executive result

The source audit confirms that the current power documentation is not yet a single consistent, implementation-ready power tree.

1. M2.9-I assigns six independent MEE1S2405SC isolated 5 V converters to the six AMC1311B voltage channels.
2. M2.6 describes each AMC1311B isolated channel as MEE1S2405SC 5 V followed by a TLV70033 3.3 V regulator, while M2.9-I describes the AMC1311B supply as 5 V. This is a source-level discrepancy requiring reconciliation against the actual schematic and exact AMC1311B supply pins.
3. M2.9-J states TMCS1123 VS -> 3V3_MEAS. That connection is incompatible with the M2.9-M preferred *study candidate* TMCS1123A1A when symmetric ±70 A linear measurement is required: TI's Rev D range is ±96 A at 5 V but −96 A to +28 A at 3.3 V.
4. The six isolated MEE1S2405SC outputs are explicitly independent floating domains for voltage measurement. They must not be borrowed, paralleled, or repurposed as a common sensor/FDA supply.
5. A dedicated low-voltage 5 V rail for the six current sensors and their analogue interface is therefore a plausible architecture to evaluate, but it is **not designed, selected, budgeted or approved** by this audit.

No schematic, BOM, net, pin mapping, PCB, firmware or production baseline is changed.

## 3. Manufacturer constraints and preliminary load arithmetic

### 3.1 TMCS1123A1A candidate

TI TMCS1123 Rev D:
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf

- Supply operating range: 3 V to 5.5 V.
- TI product data reports maximum quiescent supply current of 14 mA.
- For six sensors, a conservative first-pass sensor-only current allowance is:
  - 6 × 14 mA = **84 mA**.
  - At 5 V: **0.42 W**.
- This is only the six-sensor load. It excludes THP210, ADC/reference circuitry, MCU, diagnostics, regulator losses, startup/transient margin and any other loads.
- The A1A ±96 A linear range at 5 V is not a thermal current approval. The independent SOA and 32 A RMS continuous review in M2.9-M remains mandatory.

### 3.2 THP210 interface candidate

TI THP210 Rev C:
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/thp210.pdf

- TI product data lists 0.95 mA typical quiescent current per channel.
- Six channels correspond to **5.7 mA typical** if one THP210 is used per sensor.
- This is not a worst-case supply budget. A guaranteed maximum over operating conditions and the actual 5 V operating point must be taken from the complete electrical-characteristics table before rail sizing.
- THP210 remains a simulation candidate. Its input differential absolute maximum, common-mode range, output swing, stability and power-sequencing constraints remain open.

### 3.3 What can and cannot be concluded

The 84 mA sensor-only figure is a useful minimum planning input, not a regulator rating. Adding 5.7 mA typical for six THP210s does not create a valid worst-case rail budget. No regulator, inductor, protection, filter, connector, copper width or thermal rating is selected here.

## 4. Power-domain architecture to evaluate

The next controlled study should compare these options without changing the schematic:

**Option A — dedicated shared 5V_MEAS rail**
- Derived from the protected 24V_SELV source using a suitable non-isolated conversion stage.
- Feeds only the six TMCS1123A1A VS pins and, if confirmed by the analogue design, the six THP210 channels.
- Uses an explicitly defined measurement-ground return and return-current strategy.
- Must not connect to ISO_GND_A1..B3 or collapse the six AMC1311B floating domains.
- Requires regulator stability, EMI, ripple/noise, transient, startup, brownout, short-circuit and thermal analysis.

**Option B — separate sensor and FDA rails**
- Sensor supply and FDA supply are generated separately if noise coupling, sequencing or fault containment justify the added complexity.
- Requires a demonstrated benefit and full load/thermal analysis; do not add rails merely by preference.

**Rejected shortcut**
- Do not repurpose or parallel the six MEE1S2405SC isolated outputs.
- Do not connect their floating returns together.
- Do not simply rename 3V3_MEAS to 5V_MEAS or change a symbol/net label without tracing all loads, returns and protection paths.

No option is selected by this document.

## 5. Required evidence before rail selection

1. Read the actual current MASTER and review schematic power tree, including all regulator symbols, pins, net labels, grounds and power flags.
2. Reconcile the M2.6/M2.9-I AMC1311B 5 V versus 3.3 V supply discrepancy against the exact AMC1311B datasheet and actual implementation.
3. Produce a load table for all six TMCS1123A1A devices, all six THP210 devices (if retained), references, ADC inputs, diagnostics and any shared loads. Include min/typ/max, startup and fault states.
4. Confirm the exact TI maximum supply-current conditions for TMCS1123 and THP210 at the chosen supply and temperature.
5. Establish allowable rail ripple/noise and sensor-to-sensor coupling from the current-measurement error and phase budgets.
6. Compare 24 V-to-5 V conversion options using input range, efficiency, thermal loss, switching spectrum, isolation needs, protection and availability. No MPN is preselected.
7. Define measurement ground ownership and its relationship to AGND_A/DGND_A, AGND_B/DGND_B, MCU ground, CAN isolation and chassis/PE. Verify that the intended domains are not inadvertently bridged.
8. Review power-up, power-down, brownout, sensor-powered/FDA-unpowered and FDA-powered/sensor-unpowered states, including THP210 input clamp current and ADC protection.
9. Validate the complete six-channel network by simulation and bench testing under a separately approved safe low-voltage test plan.
