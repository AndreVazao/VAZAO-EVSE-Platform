## 1. Executive finding

The exact production MASTER has now been exported by KiCad 10.0.8 and inspected. The native netlist confirms that the documented power architecture is **not yet implemented as connected circuitry in this MASTER snapshot**.

- All six MEE1S2405SC converter outputs are unconnected. Four ISO5 labels are attached to converter VIN+ pins, not VOUT pins; two converter instances have all pins unconnected.
- All six AMC1311B devices U3–U8 have all eight pins unconnected in the exported MASTER.
- All six TMCS1123 current sensors have IN+ unconnected. IN− and GND are unconnected on five channels; on U19, IN− is tied to ADC U2 DOUT and GND is tied to ADC U2 CS through suspicious nets. VOUT is unconnected on all six; VS is unconnected on five, while U19 VS is tied to U2 SCLK through a suspicious net.
- Each TMCS1123 VREF pin is connected to a net labelled as the phase current return `*_I_RET`. VREF is not a current-return pin.
- Three multi-node nets connect U19 pins GND, IN− and VS to digital pins of ADS131M06 U2 (CS, DOUT, SCLK). These are explicitly blocked.
- `24V_SELV`, `3V3_MEAS`, `AGND_B`, `ISO5_A3` and `ISO5_B3` are dangling labels in the MASTER ERC; they are not evidence of connected supply nets.
- REF1 has VDD and VREF unconnected; only its GND pin resolves to `AGND_A`.

Therefore no valid current-sensor supply, isolated voltage-channel supply, ADC analogue supply/reference or complete measurement return network can be derived from the present MASTER. Do not select or size a 5 V regulator from this netlist.

## 5. Three blocked U19-to-U2 cross-connections

The KiCad netlist contains exactly three multi-node nets:

| Net | U19 endpoint | U2 endpoint | Electrical conflict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Net-(U19-GND)` | Pin 3 GND | Pin 17 CS | Sensor ground tied to ADC chip-select input |
| `Net-(U19-IN-)` | Pin 2 IN− | Pin 20 DOUT | Sensor current input tied to ADC digital output |
| `Net-(U19-VS)` | Pin 8 VS | Pin 19 SCLK | Sensor supply tied to ADC serial clock |

The IN−/DOUT connection is output-to-output according to the current symbol electrical types, and is also physically incompatible with the intended analogue/digital domains. All three nets are blocked. Do not restore, accept, suppress or relabel them without a separately approved pin-to-net review.

## 6. AMC1311B voltage channels and ADC/reference status

All eight pins of each AMC1311B U3–U8 are unconnected in the MASTER netlist:
- VINP, VINN
- GND1, VDD1
- VOUTP, VOUTN
- GND2, VDD2

This means the six isolated voltage-measurement front ends are not electrically connected in the current MASTER, despite being described in architecture documents.

For ADS131M06 U1, all 11 pins present in the current symbol/netlist (AIN0P/N, AIN1P/N, AIN2P/N, CS, DRDY, SCLK, DOUT, DIN) are unconnected. For U2, AIN0P/N, AIN1P/N, AIN2P/N, DRDY and DIN are unconnected; CS, SCLK and DOUT are involved in the three suspicious U19 cross-connections. The current symbol/netlist export does not expose a complete ADC pin/power model for all package pins, so this report does not infer unlisted ADC supply connections.

REF1 (`ADC_REFERENCE_OPEN`): VDD pin 1 unconnected; GND pin 2 on `/AGND_A`; VREF pin 3 unconnected. `AGND_A` therefore has only REF1 pin 2 as its observed net node in this export.

## 9. Engineering disposition and next steps

1. Freeze this MASTER netlist/ ERC as a baseline. Do not edit production wiring based on inferred intentions.
2. Reconcile the M2.6/M2.9-I discrepancy for AMC1311B supply pins against the exact TI datasheet and the intended isolation architecture. The current MASTER does not connect any AMC1311B pin.
3. Rebuild the power tree as an explicit review-only pin-to-net proposal: 24 V input/protection, each isolated converter VIN pair, each isolated output/return, AMC1311B side supplies, ADC/reference rails, current-sensor rail and analogue ground domains.
4. Separately define the six TMCS1123 pin assignments. VREF must not be used as a current-return label; VS/GND must receive verified supply/return nets; VOUT/VREF must feed the selected analogue front end; IN+/IN− are the primary current path.
5. Review and remove the three U19/U2 cross-domain connections only after the full channel-to-ADC mapping and pin-function matrix are approved.
6. Re-run native KiCad ERC/netlist after every review-only correction; compare pin/net deltas against this immutable baseline.
7. Only after the power tree is connected and reviewed, calculate the six-sensor/FDA load, regulator efficiency/thermal loss, rail ripple/noise, startup/brownout and fault sequencing.
8. Revisit TMCS1123 variant selection (A1A at 5 V remains only a candidate from M2.9-M) after the schematic and analog topology are corrected.
