## 6. Manufacturer datasheet cross-check (official TI sources)

Reviewed the official Texas Instruments product pages and datasheets for the exact orderable part numbers:

- **U19 — TMCS1123A3AQDVGR:** TI identifies this orderable as the DVG 10-pin SOIC package. The official datasheet pin table confirms pin 2 = IN−, pin 3 = GND and pin 8 = VS. Source: https://www.ti.com/product/TMCS1123/part-details/TMCS1123A3AQDVGR and https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf (Rev. D, SBOSA24D).
- **U2 — ADS131M06IPBSR:** TI identifies this orderable as the PBS 32-pin TQFP package. The official datasheet PBS pin configuration confirms pin 17 = CS, pin 19 = SCLK and pin 20 = DOUT. Source: https://www.ti.com/product/ADS131M06/part-details/ADS131M06IPBSR and https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf (SBAS949A).

This independently confirms the manufacturer-assigned package pin functions for all six pin numbers involved. It therefore strengthens the finding that the three exported nets join unlike functional signals:
- U19 GND (pin 3) ↔ U2 CS (pin 17)
- U19 IN− (pin 2) ↔ U2 DOUT (pin 20)
- U19 VS (pin 8) ↔ U2 SCLK (pin 19)

**Important boundary:** this datasheet check does not yet verify the repository's assigned footprint pad numbering, symbol-to-footprint mapping, PCB pad connectivity, native schematic wire/label endpoints, or intended circuit topology. It does not authorize a wiring correction. Those remain mandatory review gates. The status remains **BLOCKED — evidence review only**.

## 9. Documented functional-contract cross-check — intended U19/U2 roles

The repository's earlier engineering contracts were inspected to establish documented functional intent independently of the suspect MASTER geometry. Important qualification: M2.9-L-INTENDED-INTERFACE-REGISTER explicitly classifies the legacy channel assignments as source-declared functional intent, **not yet pin-level approved**. M2.9-L-REAL-ELECTRICAL-CONNECTIVITY-IMPLEMENTATION is an engineering implementation contract, not evidence that the present MASTER has been corrected or approved for release:

- `hardware/teststation/V1/CAD/verification/M2.9-J-REAL-POINT-TO-POINT-WIRING-CONTRACT.md` identifies U2 as ADC-B (ADS131M06) and explicitly assigns U19 = TMCS1123-B2 -> ADC-B CH4. It lists the six current channels as U15/U16/U17 for Zone A and U18/U19/U20 for Zone B.
- `hardware/teststation/V1/CAD/verification/M2.9-H-CONNECTED-NET-POWER-BASELINE.md` independently defines Zone B phase-2 current as TMCS1123-B2 -> ADC-B AIN4, with the sensor's VOUT feeding the ADC and sensor GND/VS on the measurement supply domain.
- `hardware/teststation/V1/CAD/verification/M2.9-E-PIN-TO-NET-FREEZE.md` defines the exact ADS131M06 control pins: pin 17 CS, pin 19 SCLK, pin 20 DOUT; its TMCS1123 pin table defines pin 2 IN−, pin 3 GND and pin 8 VS. The ADC pin map assigns AIN4P/AIN4N to pins 5/6.
- `docs/02-architecture/TS-V1-M2.4-MPN-FREEZE-FIRST-IMPLEMENTATION-PACKAGE.md` specifies the functional current-sensor interface as TMCS1123 OUT -> input filter -> ADS131M06 differential input, and states that the TMCS1123 signal/control side and ADC do not require an additional galvanic barrier between them.

The documented functional contract is incompatible with treating the three observed direct joins as the U19-B2 measurement path:
- U19 pin 3 GND must belong to the measurement reference domain, not ADC-B CS (U2 pin 17).
- U19 pin 2 IN− is the sensor's measured-current conductor terminal, not ADC-B DOUT (U2 pin 20).
- U19 pin 8 VS is the sensor supply input, not ADC-B SCLK (U2 pin 19).
- The documented sensor output path is U19 VOUT (pin 6) through the analogue input network to ADC-B CH4 (AIN4P/AIN4N, physical ADC pins 5/6), subject to final differential-interface implementation.

**New finding:** the direct endpoint overlaps are not consistent with the documented functional assignment for U19-B2 and ADC-B. This establishes a confirmed disagreement between the native MASTER connectivity and the repository's documented point-to-point/functional contract. Because the M2.9-L intent register says its channel assignments are not yet pin-level approved, this is a contract mismatch requiring design-owner confirmation—not yet authority to edit the circuit or claim an approved final topology. The exact safe corrected schematic remains undefined.
