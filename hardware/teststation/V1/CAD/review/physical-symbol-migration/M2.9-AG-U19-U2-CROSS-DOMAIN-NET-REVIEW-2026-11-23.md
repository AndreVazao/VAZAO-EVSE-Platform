## 6. Manufacturer datasheet cross-check (official TI sources)

Reviewed the official Texas Instruments product pages and datasheets for the exact orderable part numbers:

- **U19 — TMCS1123A3AQDVGR:** TI identifies this orderable as the DVG 10-pin SOIC package. The official datasheet pin table confirms pin 2 = IN−, pin 3 = GND and pin 8 = VS. Source: https://www.ti.com/product/TMCS1123/part-details/TMCS1123A3AQDVGR and https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf (Rev. D, SBOSA24D).
- **U2 — ADS131M06IPBSR:** TI identifies this orderable as the PBS 32-pin TQFP package. The official datasheet PBS pin configuration confirms pin 17 = CS, pin 19 = SCLK and pin 20 = DOUT. Source: https://www.ti.com/product/ADS131M06/part-details/ADS131M06IPBSR and https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf (SBAS949A).

This independently confirms the manufacturer-assigned package pin functions for all six pin numbers involved. It therefore strengthens the finding that the three exported nets join unlike functional signals:
- U19 GND (pin 3) ↔ U2 CS (pin 17)
- U19 IN− (pin 2) ↔ U2 DOUT (pin 20)
- U19 VS (pin 8) ↔ U2 SCLK (pin 19)

**Important boundary:** this datasheet check does not yet verify the repository's assigned footprint pad numbering, symbol-to-footprint mapping, PCB pad connectivity, native schematic wire/label endpoints, or intended circuit topology. It does not authorize a wiring correction. Those remain mandatory review gates. The status remains **BLOCKED — evidence review only**.
