# M2.9-AA — AMC1311B Isolated Power Supply Requirements and Selection Gate

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** REQUIREMENTS GATE — NOT A COMPONENT SELECTION OR APPROVED POWER DESIGN  
**Scope:** Intended six dedicated high-side power domains for voltage channels U3–U8  
**Previous checkpoint:** M2.9-Z source-declared channel intent merged; current MASTER remains unconnected.

## 1. Purpose and current evidence

Repository source documents recover six voltage channels and the legacy intent for dedicated isolated supplies U9–U14. The actual native MASTER/netlist audit does not show implemented connections for AMC1311B U3–U8 or their converter output rails. Legacy MEE1S2405SC cannot be carried forward as a safety-isolation element.

This document converts the next component-selection step into requirements and gates. It does not choose a converter or declare any safety rating.

## 2. Architecture baseline for evaluation

- Six channels: U3–U8, source-declared Zone A/B L1/L2/L3 voltage sensing.
- Six independent floating power domains remain the conservative reference for the fault-containment review, not a frozen implementation.
- One converter per channel is the baseline candidate for evaluation; any grouping requires separate documented approval.
- Low-side input source is expected to be from the system auxiliary supply, but its source/protection/voltage envelope is **OPEN**.
- High-side nominal rail is **OPEN** until the exact AMC1311B variant, sensor/interface design, loading and transient requirements are verified from datasheets and design analysis. Do not assume 5 V solely because the old converter produced 5 V.
- No old MEE1S2405SC safety-isolation role retained.

## 4. Safety-rating interpretation rules

1. A short-duration production Hi-Pot value (for example, 1 second) does not establish continuous working voltage.
2. A component isolation rating/certificate does not automatically establish the TestStation's system-level rating.
3. Do not infer reinforced insulation, measurement category, CAT rating, or clearance/creepage from nominal mains voltage.
4. Each isolated power crossing is a parallel galvanic path to the AMC1311B signal barrier; both must be assessed against the derived protection architecture.
5. The complete PCB, connectors, sensor wiring, shields, PE/chassis, service interfaces and communication interfaces must be included in the crossing/bypass review.
6. Do not use a DC/DC module as the sole safety barrier unless its exact MPN certification and the complete system design establish suitability.
7. A converter that is marketed as isolated or lists a Hi-Pot test value is not automatically eligible.

## 5. Required electrical budget per channel

Create a channel-by-channel budget only after exact parts and interface are verified:

- AMC1311B high-side supply min/typ/max, operating conditions and decoupling.
- Sensor/input network and protection load, including worst-case fault/current.
- Additional high-side loads, if any; currently **none approved**.
- Converter output tolerance, ripple, transient, startup and temperature.
- Total current and power at worst-case conditions.
- Converter efficiency, input current and auxiliary rail loading.
- Low-side and high-side decoupling placement.
- Brownout/reset/fault detection and recovery strategy.
- Thermal margin, PCB copper and ambient.

The budget must include margin and evidence, not nominal arithmetic alone.

## 6. Candidate evaluation sequence

1. Freeze product boundary/intended use and applicable standards route.
2. Define continuous working voltage, transient/impulse, OVC, pollution degree, material group, altitude and required insulation type.
3. Confirm exact AMC1311B orderable variant, high-side supply limits, and input range from the current manufacturer's datasheet.
4. Determine system auxiliary source range, protection and fault envelope.
5. Define per-channel high-side loads and rail budget.
6. Search candidate DC/DC modules using mandatory filters; capture exact datasheets/certificates/MPNs and all rating conditions.
7. Reject any candidate that fails a mandatory condition or has missing evidence.
8. Compare surviving candidates for cost, footprint, availability, EMI, thermal and layout.
9. Complete independent review of isolation crossings and PCB layout.
10. Only after approval, create a separate implementation PR for schematic/BOM changes using native KiCad; run ERC/netlist/connectivity review before any PCB/fabrication or energization.

## 8. Decision

**No converter selected.** Six independent floating supplies remain a candidate architecture for evaluating channel fault containment. This document does not approve converter voltage, power, MPN, insulation class, certification, channel grouping, or a system safety claim. The legacy MEE1S2405SC is not approved for safety-isolation use.
