# TestStation V1 — dual-domain supply architecture study

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** ARCHITECTURE CANDIDATE / SAFETY AND POWER-DOMAIN HOLD  
**Inputs:** actual MASTER netlist M2.9-P; gap matrix M2.9-Q; M2.6, M2.9-I/J/M  
**No production connectivity or component selection is approved.**

## 1. Executive decision

The actual MASTER netlist has no functioning power tree. The first architecture task is not choosing a regulator; it is preserving the galvanic barriers while supplying both sides of each AMC1311B and the low-voltage current-sensing/ADC chain.

TI AMC1311B has two separate supply domains:
- VDD1/GND1: high-side supply and return, pins 1/4.
- VDD2/GND2: low-side supply and return, pins 8/5.
- AMC1311B recommended range: 3.0–5.5 V on each side.
- TI typical test/application condition: 5 V high side and 3.3 V low side.

A single isolated 5 V output followed by a local LDO does **not** create a second isolated supply domain. If the LDO output is connected to VDD2 while its ground remains connected to the same MEE output return as GND1, the isolation barrier is bypassed. The M2.6 supply drawing is ambiguous and cannot be implemented as written until both sides are explicitly mapped.

TI source: AMC1311x datasheet Rev C, Sections 3, 5, 6 and 7.3:
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/amc1311.pdf

## 2. Manufacturer constraints

### 2.1 AMC1311B

| Item | Manufacturer data | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| VDD1/GND1 | High-side supply, 3.0–5.5 V | Must remain in the high-side floating domain |
| VDD2/GND2 | Low-side supply, 3.0–5.5 V | Must use the ADC/low-voltage side domain |
| Typical supply condition | VDD1 = 5 V, VDD2 = 3.3 V | Consistent with a 5 V isolated high-side supply plus separately referenced 3.3 V low-side supply |
| Isolation barrier | Reinforced isolation between high and low sides | Do not connect the two grounds through an LDO or shared return |

### 2.2 MEE1S2405SC

Murata product data:
- Input: 21.6–26.4 V (24 V nominal).
- Output: 5 V.
- Maximum output: 1 W / 200 mA.
- Isolation rating in product data: 1000 V.
- Unregulated isolated single-output converter.

Source: Murata MEE1S2405SC product datasheet:
https://www.murata.com/en-global/api/pdfdownloadapi?cate=cgsubPowerIsoDcDc&partno=MEE1S2405SC

The six-converter architecture is a documented intent, not a validated safety solution. The 1000 V isolation figure is not by itself proof of adequate working voltage, surge withstand, reinforced insulation, creepage/clearance or certification for the 230/400 V measurement environment. Obtain the current approval sheet and perform insulation coordination before retaining this part for any high-side floating domain.

### 2.3 TLV70033

TI lists the TLV700 family as a 200 mA LDO with 2–5.5 V input range and 3.3 V fixed option:
https://www.ti.com/product/TLV700

The LDO is not an isolation component. Its input and output share the same ground reference. It may regulate a rail within one domain; it cannot create a second galvanically isolated domain.

                                                |

Key constraints:
- Each AMC1311B VDD1/GND1 pair must remain on its own intended floating high-side domain.
- Each AMC1311B VDD2/GND2 pair must be supplied from the low-side domain associated with its ADC.
- Do not connect a TLV70033 output to VDD2 if its input return is still the VDD1/GND1 floating return.
- Decide whether low-side ADC/AMC domains A and B share one 3.3 V supply and return or remain separate. M2.9-J names AGND_A/AGND_B and DGND_A/DGND_B but does not complete the ownership/connection rules.
- The six TMCS1123 channels are low-side measurement circuits on the sensor's isolated output side. If A1A is retained, a dedicated 5V_MEAS candidate is required for symmetric ±70 A linear measurement; it is not derived from the six isolated AMC supplies.
- The 5V_MEAS source, ground ownership, FDA loads, ADC loads, transient/noise requirements and protection are all open.

## 4. Why the M2.6 LDO drawing is on HOLD

M2.6 §17 shows:
`24V -> MEE1S2405SC -> +5V_ISO-x -> TLV70033 -> +3V3_ISO-x -> AMC1311B`

This line does not say whether:
- +5V_ISO-x powers VDD1/GND1 and +3V3_ISO-x is intended for VDD2/GND2 (which would be invalid if the LDO shares the same return); or
- both rails are on the same high-side domain and the low-side VDD2/GND2 supply is missing; or
- the LDO is intended for another load.

M2.9-I §4 only says ISO_5V_x / ISO_GND_x -> AMC1311B and likewise omits the second supply domain. M2.9-J says ADC AVDD/DVDD -> 3V3_MEAS but does not explicitly assign AMC1311B VDD2/GND2. The MASTER netlist has all pins of U3–U8 unconnected. No implementation can be approved from these prose descriptions.

## 5. Current sensor / FDA rail planning

M2.9-M identifies TMCS1123A1A at 5 V as the candidate that meets the symmetric ±70 A linear envelope among the compared variants.

First-pass load arithmetic:
- Six TMCS1123 sensors × 14 mA maximum quiescent current = 84 mA sensor-only.
- At 5 V, sensor-only planning load = 0.42 W.
- Six THP210 × 0.95 mA typical = 5.7 mA typical; this is not a guaranteed maximum.
- This excludes FDA output load, ADC/reference, diagnostics, MCU, regulator losses, startup, brownout and fault transients.

Therefore this is not a rail rating. No buck/LDO MPN, inductor, protection, filter, output capacitance, connector or copper rating is selected. The 5V_MEAS candidate must be analysed for switching noise, sensor-to-sensor coupling, ADC/THP210 PSRR, ground return, sequencing and thermal behaviour.

1. Obtain the latest MEE1S2405SC approval sheet and determine whether it can provide the required working voltage and insulation class for each AMC1311B high-side domain. If not, screen appropriately rated isolated converters.
2. Draw a six-channel AMC1311B dual-domain block diagram with separate VDD1/GND1 and VDD2/GND2 rails, including the isolation boundary. Keep it review-only.
3. Reconcile the M2.6/M2.9-I supply discrepancy and document the exact intended role of every TLV70033.
4. Define whether the low-side measurement domains A/B are common or separate; use ADC reference, ground and EMC requirements to decide.
5. Define the 24V_SELV input source and protected distribution.
6. Screen a separate 24V-to-5V_MEAS converter for the six TMCS1123A1A and six THP210 channels, with a full worst-case load and noise budget.
7. Build power sequencing/fault tables: source absent, one isolated converter failed, one side powered without the other, brownout, LDO short/open, sensor/FDA partial power and ADC reset.
8. Only after review approval, make a separate review-only KiCad change, export netlist/ERC and verify that no isolation boundary is bypassed.
