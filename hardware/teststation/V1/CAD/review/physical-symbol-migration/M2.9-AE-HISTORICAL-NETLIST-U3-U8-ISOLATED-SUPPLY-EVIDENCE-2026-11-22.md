- U10 pin 1: `/ISO5_A1`
- U11 pin 1: `/ISO5_A2`

U9 pin 1 and U12 pin 1 are unconnected. For all six converters, pins 2 (VIN−), 3 (VOUT−) and 4 (VOUT+) are reported as unconnected single-node nets. The named VIN+ entries are themselves single-node nets; they do not show a completed input circuit or a connected output rail.

This artifact does not demonstrate six functioning isolated supplies or six powered high-side domains. Four input-positive labels alone are not power paths. MEE1S2405SC remains unapproved as a safety isolation barrier, consistent with prior project decisions.
