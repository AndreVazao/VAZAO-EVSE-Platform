# M2.9-AH — six-channel TMCS1123 native-netlist connectivity audit

**Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Source:** `hardware/teststation/V1/CAD/verification/netlist/M2.9-K-MASTER.net`  
**Netlist blob SHA:** `99627d6676a53b69af2a9b8d3e6f23104c423a2f`

**Scope:** read-only inspection of the existing exported netlist; no schematic, netlist, PCB or BOM changes.

## 1. Purpose

After the U19/U2 topology audit, inspect all six current-sensor instances (U15–U20) rather than treating U19 as an isolated exception. This report records what the existing exported KiCad model says, distinguishes symbol-model limitations from physical package facts, and identifies the evidence needed before any repair.

## 2. Summary

The exported netlist does **not** demonstrate six functioning current-measurement paths.

- U15, U16, U17, U18 and U20 have only pin 5 (VREF) assigned to a named net; their modeled pins 1–4 and 6–8 are exported as unconnected.
- U19 pin 5 (VREF) is assigned to `/B_L2_I_RET`; pins 2, 3 and 8 are instead joined to U2's DOUT, CS and SCLK pins respectively; pins 1, 4, 6 and 7 are unconnected.
- In all six instances, modeled pin 6 (VOUT) is unconnected. Therefore the exported netlist contains no TMCS1123 VOUT-to-ADC signal path for any of the six channels.
- The MASTER's cached TMCS1123 symbol models only pins 1–8 and omits physical pins 9 (VOC) and 10 (NC), already confirmed by the physical pin cross-check. The six-symbol model is incomplete for the exact 10-pin DVG orderable part.
- The ADC cached symbol has a known physical pin mapping defect for pins 1–6. Do not interpret its current pin-to-channel assignments as a valid physical mapping.

These are findings about the exact exported netlist and its symbol models. They do not prove what the original designer intended or define the corrected circuit.

## 5. Contract comparison

The repository documents the following functional intent:
- M2.9-J: U15/U16/U17 → ADC-A current channels 3/4/5; U18/U19/U20 → ADC-B current channels 3/4/5.
- M2.9-E: TMCS1123 pin 6 VOUT is `CURRENT_VOUT_x`; pin 5 VREF is `CURRENT_VREF_x`; pin 8 VS is named `SENSOR_5V_x`.
- M2.9-I: local TMCS1123 supply decoupling baseline 100 nF + 1 µF; family operating range 3–5.5 V.
- M2.9-J/M2.4: sensor supply baseline is named `3V3_MEAS`; sensor output proceeds through a symmetrical RC anti-alias network to the ADS131M06 differential input.
- M2.9-L intended-interface register explicitly warns that channel assignments extracted from legacy notes are source-declared functional intent, not yet pin-level approved.

The netlist therefore conflicts with the documented current-channel functional path at least in the following directly observable respects: VOUT is unconnected on all six channels; the modeled GND/VS/IN pins are not populated into a complete sensor circuit; and U19 has three direct joins to unrelated ADC digital/control pins. The VREF net naming/connection requires clarification rather than an automatic correction.

## 6. Additional supply-name clarification

The M2.9-E prototype table calls pin 8 VS `SENSOR_5V_x`, whereas M2.9-J and M2.4 call the baseline rail `3V3_MEAS`. TI's 3.0–5.5 V operating range is compatible with either nominal value, but does not establish which supply the design intends. The current exported netlist has VS unconnected for five sensors and incorrectly joined for U19, so it provides no evidence of an implemented sensor rail. Resolve the canonical rail name, voltage, tolerance, source capacity and per-channel decoupling in the approved interface contract.

## 9. Cross-check against the source contracts — additional contradictions

The full source documents were re-read on `main` at base commit `847bf54ef7560dff29573a11323b7a84fca43133`. They establish functional intent more clearly than the initial audit, but they do **not** resolve the pin-level design:

- `M2.9-H` and `M2.9-J` assign U15/U16/U17 to ADC-A CH3/CH4/CH5 and U18/U19/U20 to ADC-B CH3/CH4/CH5. They require sensor VS, GND, VOUT, and diagnostic nets, but leave exact VREF/ADC− relationship to a controlled reference arrangement.
- `M2.9-E` names TMCS1123 pin 8 as `SENSOR_5V_x`; `M2.9-J` instead says VS → `3V3_MEAS`. Both voltages fall within the device's 3 V to 5.5 V operating range, but the project documents conflict on the intended rail. Neither is selected by this audit.
- `M2.9-E` maps ADS131M06 pins 3/4 to `ZONE_x_L1_IP/IN`, pins 5/6 to `ZONE_x_L2_IP/IN`, and pins 7/8 to `ZONE_x_L3_IP/IN`; `M2.9-J` describes CH3/4/5 using `Lx_I` and `Lx_I_RET`. The channel grouping aligns at a functional level, but the exact P/N polarity and net-name equivalence are not explicitly reconciled. In addition, the cached ADC symbol's known physical pin defect prevents using the current MASTER pin mapping as proof.
- `M2.9-L-INTENDED-INTERFACE-REGISTER` expressly says the source-derived mappings are not pin-level approved. `M2.9-L-REAL-ELECTRICAL-CONNECTIVITY-IMPLEMENTATION` requires actual wires and verified symbols, but is an implementation contract rather than evidence that the current MASTER meets it.
- `M2.9-I` specifies local 100 nF + 1 µF bypassing at TMCS1123 VS; this is a documented prototype baseline, not evidence of implemented capacitors in MASTER.

### Manufacturer pin-function clarification

The current TI TMCS1123 datasheet, revision D (May 2026), confirms the 10-pin DVG functions and explicitly states:
- pin 4 ALERT is open-drain, active-low; connect to GND if unused;
- pin 5 VREF is an analog zero-current reference output; leave floating if unused;
- pin 7 OC is open-drain, active-low; connect to GND if unused;
- pin 9 VOC sets the overcurrent threshold; connect to VS if unused;
- pin 10 NC is reserved and may be grounded or left floating.

Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmcs1123.pdf (TI datasheet SBOSA24D, Rev. D, May 2026).

This resolves the *permitted unused-pin treatments* for the exact device family, but it does not decide whether ALERT/OC are used, what VOC threshold is required, or whether VREF is routed to the ADC negative input. In particular, pin 5 VREF is an **output**, not a ground pin; connecting it to an `I_RET` net is only acceptable if that net is deliberately the corresponding ADC differential reference node and the complete analogue network is designed and reviewed for it.

### Revised disposition

The source contracts recover the intended six channel groups, but still do not provide an approved pin-level current-sensor-to-ADC table. The next engineering artifact should be a **requirements reconciliation matrix**, not a wiring correction: one row per U15–U20, recording current path direction, pin 5 VREF destination, pin 6 VOUT filtering and ADC P/N pair, pin 8 supply rail, pin 4/7 diagnostic policy, pin 9 VOC policy, and pin 10 NC treatment. Values or connections not explicitly approved remain OPEN.

No schematic, netlist, BOM, PCB, firmware, footprint assignment or safety classification was changed. No fabrication, certification or mains energization is approved.
