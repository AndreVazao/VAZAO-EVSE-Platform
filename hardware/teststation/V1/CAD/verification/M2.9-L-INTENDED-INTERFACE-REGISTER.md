## Evidence classification

- **Source-declared intent:** a functional note appears in the legacy drawing.
- **Exported connectivity:** a wire/label/pin relationship is present in the KiCad-generated netlist.
- **Datasheet verified:** pin function and package number have been checked against the exact orderable part and official manufacturer documentation.
- **Approved design intent:** the signal and destination are accepted in the design requirements and reviewed by the responsible engineer.

These are distinct states. A source-declared intent must not be promoted to “connected” or “verified” without further evidence.

## Open questions blocking a corrected schematic

1. What are the exact voltage/current measurement inputs and their required ranges, accuracy, bandwidth, sampling and protection?
2. What is the approved mapping of each sensor output to ADC differential input pairs, including polarity?
3. What are the six isolated supply-domain names and which circuits belong to each domain?
4. Which exact MCU pins and peripherals are assigned to each ADC and EV/CP/PP interface?
5. What is the intended physical CAN topology, including transceiver, termination, surge protection and isolation barrier?
6. What physical hardware independently removes hazardous energy on E-stop, and how is contactor feedback monitored?
7. What exact reference IC/circuit replaces `ADC_REFERENCE_OPEN`?

Until each question has evidence, the relevant connection remains **TBD**. Do not infer it from a diagram's visual proximity or connect nets merely to reduce ERC errors.
