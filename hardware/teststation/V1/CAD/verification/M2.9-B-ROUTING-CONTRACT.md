# M2.9-B — Routing Contract

## PCB-A
- Keep each AMC1311B input/output domain compact.
- Keep isolated DC/DC converter physically adjacent to its isolation boundary.
- Keep ADC analogue inputs away from MCU clocks and CAN.
- No copper crosses the isolation corridor.
- Star/reference strategy must be validated during analog review.
- Zone A and Zone B channels remain physically symmetrical where practical.

## PCB-B
- Safety MCU and E-stop logic occupy the protected control area.
- Contactor drive currents remain separated from ADC/reference routing.
- CAN isolation barrier is explicit.
- 24 V entry and transient protection stay at board edge.
- Watchdog and feedback paths must not depend on service/debug circuitry.

## PCB-C
- CP protection is at the connector entry.
- PP resistor bank is kept away from MCU analog inputs.
- CP PWM and measurement paths are short and shielded from switching nodes.
- Service connector does not bypass CP/PP protection.

## Release gate

Routing is only considered complete after:
1. verified footprints;
2. completed net assignment;
3. copper routing;
4. ERC;
5. DRC;
6. isolation review;
7. mechanical review;
8. manufacturing review.
