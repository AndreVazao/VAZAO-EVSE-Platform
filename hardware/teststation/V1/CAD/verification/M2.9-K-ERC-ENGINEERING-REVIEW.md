## Findings from the legacy source

The legacy schematic declares 24 component instances, 43 local labels, and 40 wire records. A source-level scan found at least 12 wire records whose start and end coordinates are identical (zero-length records). The file contains no `NoConn` records and no global-label records. Several labels are placed at coordinates that are not obviously at the corresponding component pins; for example, the three phase-voltage label pairs are at x=700 legacy units while U3–U8 symbols are placed at x=1200/4400/5100/8400 units. The imported native file's ERC correspondingly reports dangling labels, disconnected pins and undriven inputs.

This means the source cannot be treated as a proven, fully connected electrical baseline just because its title/comments say “REAL ELECTRICAL CONNECTIVITY BASELINE”. That claim needs to be demonstrated by net connectivity and pin-by-pin verification.

The categories are ERC messages, not necessarily independent root causes. One pin may trigger both “not connected” and “not driven”; custom-library warnings are environment/configuration issues and must not be confused with physical wiring defects.

## Required work before calling the schematic electrically complete

1. **Library migration:** convert the legacy cache library into a versioned native KiCad symbol library, register it in a project-local `sym-lib-table`, and verify that symbol pin numbers/types/positions match authoritative component datasheets. Do not just silence library warnings.
2. **Connectivity reconstruction:** make a net-by-net plan for the measurement channels (ADS131M06), isolated voltage channels (AMC1311), isolated supplies (MEE1S2405SC), current sensors (TMCS1123), MCU/SPI, isolated CAN (ISO1044), ADC reference and safety/control signals.
3. **Pin-level review:** verify every connection against each exact manufacturer part number and package. The legacy cache definitions are custom/approximate and their pin mappings must not be accepted without datasheet comparison.
4. **Conflict review:** investigate the output-to-output connection reported at U2 pin 20 (DOUT) and U19 pin 2 (IN-); determine whether the net is intended and whether either symbol pin type or wire/net mapping is wrong.
5. **ERC rerun:** after genuine fixes, rerun ERC and document any intentional exceptions with a written engineering rationale. Do not blanket-suppress or downgrade findings to force a green result.
6. **PCB/DRC:** only after schematic connectivity and component footprints are validated, create/verify the board and run DRC.
7. **Safety gate:** no fabrication or mains energization until qualified engineering review confirms isolation, creepage/clearance, protective devices, component ratings, sensing accuracy, fault handling and applicable EVSE standards.
