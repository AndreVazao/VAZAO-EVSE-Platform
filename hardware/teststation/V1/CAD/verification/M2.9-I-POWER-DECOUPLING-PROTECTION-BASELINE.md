# VAZAO EVSE TestStation V1 — M2.9-I
## Power, decoupling, protection and passive baseline

**Status:** engineering prototype baseline  
**Release class:** NOT production / NOT certification / NOT mains-energization  
**Date:** 2026-09-26

## 1. Purpose

M2.9-I closes the first explicit baseline for:
- 24 V distribution;
- six independent isolated 5 V measurement supplies;
- 3.3 V measurement/control rails;
- ADC supply/reference decoupling;
- TMCS1123 supply/diagnostic conditioning;
- CP/PP low-voltage protection;
- MCU/CAN supply protection;
- local bulk capacitance;
- service/test-point strategy.

The electrical schematic source remains an engineering baseline and is **not ERC/DRC-clean**.

## 2. Corrected channel count

The TestStation V1 requires **six** isolated voltage channels and **six** current channels:

| Zone | Voltage | Current | ADC |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Zone A | L1/L2/L3 = 3 | L1/L2/L3 = 3 | ADS131M06-A |
| Zone B | L1/L2/L3 = 3 | L1/L2/L3 = 3 | ADS131M06-B |

Therefore the implementation target is:
- AMC1311B ×6;
- MEE1S2405SC ×6;
- TMCS1123 ×6;
- ADS131M06 ×2.

No shared floating return is permitted between isolated voltage channels.

## 3. 24 V primary SELV distribution

### Input
24 V_SELV enters the TestStation through the protected service/power interface.

Baseline:
- F_24V: fuse or approved electronic protection, final rating after load calculation;
- reverse-polarity protection: TBD by final power architecture;
- TVS_24V: candidate selected after actual surge/transient environment is defined;
- C_24V_BULK: 470 µF electrolytic, voltage rating >=35 V, engineering baseline;
- C_24V_HF: 1 µF ceramic;
- C_24V_LOCAL: 100 nF ceramic at local IC/regulator entry points.

The 24 V protection values remain installation/product-specific until the complete 24 V load and fault analysis is frozen.

## 4. Six isolated measurement supplies

Each AMC1311B voltage channel receives its own isolated converter:

**24V_SELV -> MEE1S2405SC -> ISO_5V_x / ISO_GND_x -> AMC1311B**

x = A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, B3.

MEE1S2405SC baseline:
- input: 21.6–26.4 V;
- output: 5 V;
- maximum output: 200 mA;
- maximum power: 1 W;
- isolation: 1000 V;
- operating temperature: -40 to +85 °C.

These values are taken from the Murata product datasheet. citeturn0search48

### Per-channel passive baseline
At the isolated 5 V output:
- C_ISO_5V_100N = 100 nF ceramic, placed at AMC1311B supply pins;
- C_ISO_5V_1U = 1 µF ceramic, local bulk/high-frequency support;
- optional C_ISO_5V_4U7 = 4.7 µF only if converter stability/transient testing supports it.

**HOLD:** the exact output-capacitance requirement of the selected MEE1S2405SC implementation must be checked against the latest approval/application documentation before PCB release.

The six isolated outputs must remain electrically independent.

## 5. AMC1311B input network

For each voltage measurement channel:

HV measurement divider -> protection/filter -> AMC1311B input -> isolated 5 V domain.

Existing divider baseline:
- 4 × 470 kΩ series upper resistance;
- 10 kΩ lower resistance;
- values remain prototype values pending final voltage-class, tolerance, power and surge analysis.

Recommended prototype passive controls:
- series input resistor: 1 kΩ class, final value after bandwidth/error analysis;
- differential/input capacitor: 1 nF class, final value after phase-error verification;
- clamp/protection: low-capacitance protection selected for the actual AMC1311B input common-mode range.

**HOLD:** do not add arbitrary TVS devices directly to the precision input if their capacitance/leakage compromises phase measurement.

## 6. ADS131M06 supply

Each ADS131M06 uses:
- AVDD = 3.3 V_MEAS;
- DVDD = 3.3 V_MEAS;
- AGND/DGND layout according to the TI device/layout guidance;
- local 100 nF ceramic at each supply pin group;
- 1 µF local bulk ceramic per ADC;
- dedicated REFIN routing;
- local reference decoupling according to the selected reference implementation.

ADS131M06 supports 2.7–3.6 V analog and digital supplies, six simultaneous differential channels and external/internal reference operation. citeturn0search1turn0search49

### Reference
REFIN is treated as a precision analog node:
- short routing;
- no digital return sharing through the reference path;
- local decoupling;
- no service connector connection unless explicitly protected.

The final REF1 MPN and its exact decoupling network remain a controlled HOLD.

## 7. TMCS1123 current sensors

Six TMCS1123 channels:
- A1/A2/A3;
- B1/B2/B3.

The selected family supports 3–5.5 V supply operation, 80 ARMS continuous-current class, reinforced isolation and 250 kHz bandwidth. citeturn0search0turn0search5

Prototype local supply:
- 100 nF ceramic at VS;
- 1 µF ceramic local bulk;
- VREF routed separately from noisy digital returns;
- ALERT/OC routed to the safety/diagnostic MCU through defined logic protection.

**HOLD:** exact passive values around VREF, ALERT and OC shall follow the selected TMCS1123 variant/datasheet revision and the desired current range.

## 8. STM32 rails

STM32G474:
- 3.3 V MCU rail;
- 100 nF local decoupling at each supply group;
- 4.7 µF local bulk;
- separate filtering between precision measurement and general MCU supply where the final power-tree analysis shows benefit.

STM32G031:
- 3.3 V control rail;
- local 100 nF + 1 µF baseline;
- reset/boot/service networks kept away from HV measurement routing.

Final STM32 pin-by-pin power network remains subject to the exact package datasheet and KiCad symbol verification.

## 9. CAN isolation

ISO1044:
- isolated-side supply and logic-side supply explicitly separated;
- 100 nF local bypass at each supply pin group;
- 1 µF local bulk;
- CANH/CANL protection to be selected after cable length, connector and transient environment are frozen.

No CAN protection component may bridge the galvanic isolation barrier.

## 10. CP / PP protection

CP:
- PWM path remains controlled by STM32G031;
- CP feedback returns through a protected ADC/input path;
- series protection resistor and clamp network are mandatory;
- the CP line shall not be connected directly to an MCU pin without the defined interface/protection.

PP:
- resistor-bank architecture retained;
- sense input protected before MCU ADC;
- resistor tolerances shall be chosen so the Type-2 coding windows remain unambiguous after temperature and ADC error.

## 11. Safety power separation

The following functions must not depend solely on software:
- emergency energy removal;
- main contactor opening;
- load contactor opening;
- contactor feedback fault detection.

The MCU provides supervision/diagnostics, but the final E-stop energy-removal path remains independent.

## 13. PCB transfer rules

1. Precision ADC/reference traces are kept short.
2. Each isolated voltage channel retains its own floating return.
3. No copper crosses an isolation barrier unless the component itself is an approved isolation component.
4. HV sensing divider resistors remain physically distributed to control voltage stress.
5. High-current TMCS paths remain short and thermally reviewed.
6. Digital SPI routing stays outside precision analog input regions where practical.
7. Test points are provided for every supply domain without defeating isolation.
8. Creepage/clearance is calculated from the final working voltage, pollution degree, overvoltage category, material group and applicable product standard; it is not inferred from a generic PCB rule.
