# U19 / U2 — physical pin cross-check

Date: 2026-10-30  
Status: **REVIEW BLOCKED — no connectivity approval**

## Why this record exists

The KiCad master netlist exports three two-node nets between U19 (TMCS1123 symbol) and U2 (ADS131M06 symbol). Those exported pin numbers are schematic-model identifiers. They must not be confused with verified physical package pins until each symbol is checked against the exact orderable part.

The exact TI orderable code is confirmed active in the DVG 10-pin package. The existing master symbol models only pins 1–8 and omits physical pins 9 (VOC) and 10 (NC).

## U2 — ADS131M06IPBSR

The exact orderable part is the 32-pin PBS TQFP. The cached master symbol is a reduced logical abstraction and is not a complete physical package symbol. Its current logical pin labels include CS_17, SCLK_19 and DOUT_20, but these labels and numbers cannot be accepted as the physical package pin numbers without a complete datasheet-derived pin map.

| Master symbol pin | Cached logical name | Exported net | Physical package pin |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 17 | CS | Net-(U19-GND) | NOT ESTABLISHED |
| 19 | SCLK | Net-(U19-VS) | NOT ESTABLISHED |
| 20 | DOUT | Net-(U19-IN-) | NOT ESTABLISHED |

The datasheet cross-check previously established that the cached symbol's pins 1–6 also do not match the physical ADS131M06 package pin functions. Therefore no U2 physical pin assignment or corrected wiring is inferred in this record.

Reference: TI ADS131M06 datasheet, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf  
Exact orderable part: https://www.ti.com/product/ADS131M06/part-details/ADS131M06IPBSR

## Connectivity observations — not approved design intent

The generated master netlist currently reports:

- `Net-(U19-GND)`: U19 logical pin 3 ↔ U2 logical pin 17 (CS)
- `Net-(U19-IN-)`: U19 logical pin 2 ↔ U2 logical pin 20 (DOUT)
- `Net-(U19-VS)`: U19 logical pin 8 ↔ U2 logical pin 19 (SCLK)

These are facts about the current KiCad model/export only. Because the U2 symbol is not package-complete and U19's legacy pin electrical type is incorrect, they are **not sufficient evidence** to determine whether the physical circuit is wired incorrectly, whether the legacy drawing has graphical errors, or whether symbol modelling caused misleading interpretation.

2. Reconstruct the complete TMCS1123A3AQDVGR 10-pin symbol, including VOC and NC, with pin 2 correctly modelled as an analog input.
