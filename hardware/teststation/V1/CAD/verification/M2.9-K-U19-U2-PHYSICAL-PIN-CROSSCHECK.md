## Why this record exists

The KiCad master netlist exports three two-node nets between U19 (TMCS1123 symbol) and U2 (ADS131M06 symbol). Those exported pin numbers are schematic-model identifiers. They must not be confused with verified physical package pins until each symbol is checked against the exact orderable part.

## U2 — ADS131M06IPBSR

The exact orderable part is the 32-pin PBS TQFP. The cached master symbol is incomplete and has incorrect assignments for physical pins 1–6, but the TI datasheet does independently confirm that physical pins 17, 19 and 20 are CS, SCLK and DOUT respectively. The three U2 pin numbers involved in the reported nets therefore do correspond to these physical package pins.

| Master symbol pin | Cached logical name | Exported net | Physical package pin |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 17 | CS | Net-(U19-GND) | NOT ESTABLISHED |
| 19 | SCLK | Net-(U19-VS) | NOT ESTABLISHED |
| 20 | DOUT | Net-(U19-IN-) | NOT ESTABLISHED |

The datasheet cross-check establishes that the cached symbol's pins 1–6 do not match the physical ADS131M06 package pin functions (the complete 32-pin map is recorded in the component pin-verification register and enforced by the isolated draft's static checker). Pins 17, 19 and 20, however, are confirmed as CS, SCLK and DOUT. This means the three exported U19/U2 connections are genuine physical pin-number/function relationships in the current master model, not merely an ADS131M06 numbering ambiguity.

Reference: TI ADS131M06 datasheet, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m06.pdf  
Exact orderable part: https://www.ti.com/product/ADS131M06/part-details/ADS131M06IPBSR

## Connectivity observations — not approved design intent

The generated master netlist currently reports:

- `Net-(U19-GND)`: U19 logical pin 3 ↔ U2 logical pin 17 (CS)
- `Net-(U19-IN-)`: U19 logical pin 2 ↔ U2 logical pin 20 (DOUT)
- `Net-(U19-VS)`: U19 logical pin 8 ↔ U2 logical pin 19 (SCLK)

These are facts about the current KiCad model/export. The ADS131M06 physical functions at pins 17, 19 and 20 are confirmed by TI, and the TMCS1123 physical functions at pins 2, 3 and 8 are confirmed by TI. Thus the reported pairings are real package pin/function pairings: GND↔CS, IN−↔DOUT, and VS↔SCLK. They are strong evidence of incorrect connectivity or an incorrect legacy drawing, not a reason to assume the pin numbering is wrong. U19 pin 2's legacy electrical type is still incorrect (it is an analog input, not an output), but correcting that type alone would not resolve the physical connection.
