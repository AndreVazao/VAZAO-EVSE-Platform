# M2.9-F — Master Schematic Baseline

A real KiCad legacy schematic source has been committed as an **intermediate source artifact**. KiCad supports legacy `.sch` files and converts them to modern `.kicad_sch` on write; modern schematics embed their symbol information. citeturn0search12turn0search13

## Included hierarchy
- Zone A voltage/current measurement
- Zone B voltage/current measurement
- six isolated AMC1311B voltage channels
- six TMCS1123 current channels
- two ADS131M06 ADC domains
- STM32G474 measurement/control
- STM32G031 EV/safety control
- ISO1044 CAN isolation
- CP/PP
- contactor feedback and E-stop
- 24 V / isolated 5 V architecture

## Deliberate limitation
The file is a **master functional baseline**, not yet a complete symbol-instantiated schematic. It is intentionally not being represented as ERC-clean or fabrication-ready.
